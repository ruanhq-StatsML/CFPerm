\section{Scene}
One episode is one arriving user. Three offers are scored, and the beam keeps only the top offer.
\(Y=1\) when that offer activates the user.
The habit offer activates even-numbered users, so the pre-drift rate is \(0.50\).
The growth offer activates a user unless the index is a multiple of \(3\), which is two thirds of arrivals.
The click offer activates only multiples of \(5\).
Forty users, drift at user 16. Each user scores three children. The window and the column count stay fixed.

Before the drift the judge follows the calibrated score and selects habit.
After the drift, weight \(0.80\) moves onto the click score, and the judge selects click.
The growth policy uses habit through the first 12 users.
It switches the next user to the growth offer only after the mean loss of the last four finished users exceeds the reference mean loss by \(0.15\).
That switch happens at user 17.

\section{Activation}
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lrrl}
\toprule
Policy & Pre-drift & Post-drift & Reading \\
\midrule
Judge & 0.500 & 0.167 & shortfall, \(-8\) activations \\
Growth, lever at 17 & 0.500 & 0.625 & increment, \(+3\) activations \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

Against the drifted judge, the growth policy activates 11 more of the 24 post-drift users.
Against the pre-drift rate, it activates 3 more than \(0.50\times 24\).
The post-drift rate \(0.625\) is above \(0.500\), so the business line itself is an increment.
The earlier Game of 24 line could only record a shortfall, because its reference rate was already \(1\).

\section{Prompt channels}
The same comparison is applied after \(Y\) is written.
On the judge policy the activation rate falls by \(0.33\).
The conservative share rises from \(0.50\) to \(0.83\).
\(|H|\) falls from \(2.00\) to \(1.88\), and the prior falls from \(0.50\) to \(0.20\), because the selected offer changes from habit to click.
Children stay at 3.

On the growth policy the activation rate rises by \(0.125\).
The prior rises from \(0.50\) to \(0.65\), which is the growth offer's stable score taking over after user 17.
The absolute residual rises only by \(0.006\).
The conservative share falls from \(0.50\) to \(0.375\), because more users activate and \(H\) is positive on those steps.
\(|H|\) stays near \(2.00\).
Children stay at 3.

The increment here is the activation rate clearing its own pre-drift mean.
The prompt records that clearance as a higher prior and a lower conservative share, at the same scoring cost.
