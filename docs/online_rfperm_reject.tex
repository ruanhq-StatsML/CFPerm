\paragraph{Not rejection inference.}
OnlineRFPerm asks whether a frozen probe still pays rent on the new batch.
$T$ is the batch label. Complete-case MSE on shown rows is not reject inference:
we do not impute $Y$ on $S=0$. Non-finite last-column $Y$ means unshown.
A policy hop $P(S=1\mid X)$ can move complete-case MSE while $P(Y\mid X)$ holds.
IPS-weighted MSE on $S=1$ is a reweight under selection on observables,
not a unique CATE, not Shapley.
\paragraph{Read.}
Select rings $P(S\mid X)$ (labeled-slice MMD $+$ shown rate) while full-X MMD stays at the quiet floor.
Concept rings the $Y$ probes with that same quiet floor on selection and MMD.
Covariate walks $X_0$, which also feeds $S$, so both MMD and selection hop --- that is the DGP.
Complete-case MSE FAR on quiet is still twitchy; ADDIS stays the primary mark on the $Y$ stream.
