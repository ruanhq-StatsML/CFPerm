\begin{abstract}
We elaborate the method \emph{as graph shift}: compare edge-feature
laws on two windows of the $(u,i,t)$ interaction graph; measure with
conditional mean, RBF-MMD$^2$, and period PO-risk; localize by
rank-average scores on business entity keys; attribute with one FSDS
on $K^\star$. Continuous time only changes the sliding windows.
Community discovery is out of scope here.
\end{abstract}

\section{Definition}
$G_W=(V_W,E_W)$ from edges with $t\in W$; features $x_e$ from
in-window FE only.
Graph shift $= P^{W_{\mathrm{ref}}}(X)$ vs $P^{W_{\mathrm{cur}}}(X)$
(and optional $Y=\mathrm{click}$).

\section{Three rulers}
After W1-only StandardScaler:
\begin{align*}
  \delta&=\mu_{\mathrm{cur}}-\mu_{\mathrm{ref}},\\
  \widehat{\mathrm{MMD}}^2&(X_{\mathrm{ref}},X_{\mathrm{cur}}),\\
  \mathrm{PO}&:\ \mathrm{mean}(\hat\tau^2\mid v),\ \mathrm{VIMP}.
\end{align*}
Entity score $=$ rank-average of (cmean $\ell_2$, MMD$^2$, PO).

\section{Skeleton}
Monitor global $T_t^{\mathrm{MMD/PO/cmean}}$ $\to R_t$ (when).
On reject / offline two-window: score entities, Tail/CV/$R$ stop
$\to K^\star$ (where). One FSDS + signs on $K^\star$ (what).
Nest \texttt{merchant}$\to$\texttt{user}$\to\texttt{order}.

\section{Why graph shift (not point anomalies)}
Target is a distributional support block explaining $\delta$, not
isolated IDs; evidence is shape (MMD) + direction (cmean) + risk (PO).

\section{Prototype map}
\texttt{feature\_engineer} $\to$ grids;
\texttt{run\_w1w2\_mmd\_po\_localize\_fsds.py} for two-window;
continuous $=$ same scores on sliding $(t-h,t)$ vs $(t,t+\Delta t)$.

\section{Takeaway}
Graph shift measured by cmean+MMD+PO; localize by entity decomposition;
FSDS only at $K^\star$; no community objective required.
