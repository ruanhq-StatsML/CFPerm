\begin{abstract}
Attribution (cmean / FSDS / PO-VIMP) delivers tip features $\Jstar$ and
a support $K^\star$, plus a signed direction (正向/负向).
This note adds the \textbf{timing} layer: stream consecutive batches,
compute tip-group LOCO PO-risk, form
$S_t=|L_{\mathrm{tip}}(t)-\bar L_{\mathrm{burn}}|$, and run one
OnlineRFPerm scalar stream. The first FDR reject after burn-in is the
\textbf{accurate change-point} of the attributed tips---when their
PO-risk contribution collapses or rises---not merely that a global
shift occurred.
\end{abstract}

\section{Why LOCO PO-risk (not only full PO)}
Full-batch PO-risk $\mathrm{PO}_t$ fires when \emph{anything} in
$(X,y)$ degrades $\mu_{t-1}$ on batch $t$.
After attribution we already know \emph{which} tips matter; the
open question is \emph{when} those tips moved.

Leave-one-covariate-out (group) PO-risk isolates $\Jstar$:
\[
  L_{\mathrm{tip}}(t)
  \;=\;
  \mathrm{MAE}\!\big(y_t,\mu_{t-1}^{-\Jstar}(X_t)\big)
  \;-\;
  \mathrm{MAE}\!\big(y_t,\mu_{t-1}(X_t)\big),
\]
with tip columns masked to control means.
$L_{\mathrm{tip}}>0$ $\Leftrightarrow$ the attributed tips reduce probe risk
under the previous-batch fit.

Raw $L_{\mathrm{tip}}$ is often \emph{stably high} when tips always matter.
The monitor freezes burn mean $\bar L$ and forms
\[
  S_t \;=\; \bigl|L_{\mathrm{tip}}(t)-\bar L\bigr| \;+\; \mathrm{PO}_t.
\]
\textbf{Primary timing} uses a burn-calibrated hard gate
\[
  S_t \;>\; \overline{S}_{\mathrm{burn}} + k\,\mathrm{sd}(S_{\mathrm{burn}})
  \qquad (k{=}5\ \mathrm{default}).
\]
A tip mean/coef shift typically \emph{collapses} $L_{\mathrm{tip}}$ on the
transition batch while $\mathrm{PO}_t$ spikes; $S_t$ pins that batch.
OnlineRFPerm on $S_t$ / $\mathrm{PO}_t$ remains a peer FDR stream
(Lead $= t_{\mathrm{tip}}-t_{\mathrm{PO}}$).
Also log $\mathrm{sign}(L_{\mathrm{tip}}-\bar L)$ next to the
正向/负向 outcome direction.

\section{OnlineRFPerm shell (peer)}
Burn-in reference on $S_t$ $\to$ $T_t=S_t-e_{\mathrm{ref}}$ $\to$
rank/EWMA $p_t$ $\to$ online FDR.
Reported change-point defaults to the \textbf{hard gate}; RFPerm
first-reject is logged for AR comparison.
Detection delay vs known shift $t_0$:
$\mathrm{delay}=t^\star_{\mathrm{tip}}-t_0$.
\section{Protocol (locked with attribution)}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=0.35em]
  \item Localize $\to K^\star$; tip features $\Jstar$ (PO-VIMP / $|\delta|$ / FSDS).
  \item Report signed direction $\mathrm{sign}(\Delta\bar y)$, $\mathrm{sign}(\delta_j)$.
  \item Freeze $\Jstar$; start LOCO-PO OnlineRFPerm on the live batch stream.
  \item Emit $t^\star_{\mathrm{tip}}$, delay, Lead vs full-PO, tip-share traj.
\end{enumerate}
\textbf{Does not} re-enter the Drill product rule; timing is downstream
of a fixed attributed tip set.

\section{Synthetic smoke (repo)}
\texttt{scripts/run\_loco\_po\_timing\_monitor.py}: tip mean-shift +
coef flip at known batch; tip-LOCO first reject near the shift.
Module: \texttt{agod/loco\_po\_monitor.py}.

\section{Takeaway}
Attribution answers \emph{where/which/which way}.
LOCO-PO OnlineRFPerm answers \emph{when} those tips started moving
PO-risk---the accurate time point after attribution.
