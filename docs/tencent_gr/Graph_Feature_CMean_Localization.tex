\begin{abstract}
We formulate a \emph{locked} localization protocol for period-to-period
shift on \textbf{graph-derived tabular features} only.
Pipeline:
\[
  \underbrace{X}_{\text{graph features}}
  \;\xrightarrow{\;\mathrm{StandardScaler}(W_1)\;}
  \underbrace{(\delta,\,D,\,r)}_{\text{conditional means}}
  \;\xrightarrow{\;\text{early stop}\;}
  K^\star
  \;\xrightarrow{\;\text{one FSDS}\;}
  \text{feature ranking}.
\]
Here \(K^\star\) is merely the final edge/entity support after cmean
row-spectrum shrinkage---not a community, ego-net, or graph-algorithm
object. The protocol is modality-agnostic, non-causal
(\(W=\) period), and not subgroup analysis (retrieval kernel, not
\(\tau(S)\)). Out of scope: community detection, ego-centric networks,
ULS/PPR-Nibble/GraphScan, GNN attribution.
\end{abstract}

%======================================================================
\section{Justification (required)}
\label{sec:justify}

\paragraph{Target.}
Localize where period mean-shift of $X$ concentrates by shrinking
support $S_{t+1}\subset S_t$; deliver a retrieval set $K^\star$.

\paragraph{Not claimed.}
No treatment effect; $W\in\{0,1\}$ is period ($W_1$/$W_2$), not
treatment. Drill gates are descriptive thresholds, not $\alpha$-tests.

\paragraph{Looks like subgroup analysis; is not.}
Control flow shrinks support data-dependently (same \emph{shape} as
post-hoc subgroups). The functional differs:

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}lll@{}}
\toprule
 & Subgroup analysis & This protocol \\
\midrule
Object & $\tau(S)$ & $\delta(S)=\mu_S^{(1)}-\mu_S^{(0)}$ on $X$ \\
Role of set & inferential subgroup & retrieval / delivery support \\
``Better'' & larger / significant effect
  & sharper $r$ / lower residual $R$ \\
Justify & identification + selective inference
  & stable retrieval + business cap + descriptive gates \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\noindent
Write-time checklist (J1--J8): task $=$ period-shift localization;
entity key atoms; column vs row split; stop $=$ flat rows / business
cap / rejected $R$; deliver $\pi$-stable sets; Lift (if any) $=$
enrichment $\bar s_S/\bar s_C$; optional holdout; business granularity
cap. Forbidden phrases: ``larger effect'', ``significant lift as ATE'',
``detected community''.

%======================================================================
\section{Setup: graph features and periods}
\label{sec:setup}

Each row $i=1,\ldots,n$ is an edge (interaction) with
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em]
  \item $X_i\in\R^d$: \textbf{graph features} only
    (tabular aggregates from the interaction graph---not a graph
    algorithm pass);
  \item $W_i\in\{0,1\}$: period ($0=W_1$, $1=W_2$);
  \item entity key $e_i$ (e.g.\ merchant / user / order id).
\end{itemize}
Scaler for $X$ is fit on $W_1$ only. Parent support $S\subseteq\{1,\ldots,n\}$
starts as all edges (or a mass-filtered parent).

\begin{definition}[Mass gate]
\[
  U_{\mathrm{ok}}
  =\bigl\{
    v:\ n_1(v)\ge n_{\min},\ n_2(v)\ge n_{\min}
  \bigr\},
\]
where $n_w(v)=\#\{i\in S: e_i=v,\,W_i=w\}$.
\end{definition}

%======================================================================
\section{Primitive: conditional means}
\label{sec:cmean}

\begin{definition}[Parent gap and entity--feature matrix]
\begin{align}
  \mu_S^{(w)}
  &=\E[X\mid i\in S,\,W=w],
  &
  \delta(S)
  &=\mu_S^{(1)}-\mu_S^{(0)}\in\R^d,
  \label{eq:delta}\\
  \mu_v^{(w)}
  &=\E[X\mid e=v,\,i\in S,\,W=w],
  &
  D_{v:}
  &=\mu_v^{(1)}-\mu_v^{(0)}\in\R^d.
  \label{eq:D}
\end{align}
Stacking $v\in U_{\mathrm{ok}}$ gives $D\in\R^{|U_{\mathrm{ok}}|\times d}$:
\textbf{rows $=$ entities, columns $=$ features}.
\end{definition}

\begin{remark}
All gates below are projections of the same $(\delta,D)$. No MMD
leave-one-entity loop; optional MMD is audit-only on few candidates.
\end{remark}

%======================================================================
\section{Column view: feature guidance}
\label{sec:cols}

\begin{definition}[Feature shares]
\[
  \mathrm{share}_j(S)
  =\frac{\delta_j(S)^2}{\norm{\delta(S)}^2+\varepsilon},
  \qquad
  J^\star(S)=\mathrm{TopShare}\bigl(\mathrm{share}(S)\bigr).
\]
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Gates G0--G2 (not drill)]
\begin{description}[leftmargin=2.2em]
  \item[G0] $\norm{\delta(S)}<\varepsilon_\delta$: skip FSDS if stop here.
  \item[G1] large $\norm{\delta}$ + concentrated share:
    if stop at $S$, run FSDS once; report $J^\star$.
  \item[G2] large $\norm{\delta}$ + flat share: weak tip; FSDS optional.
\end{description}
\end{definition}
\noindent
Allowed: ``shifted / not'', ``watch $J^\star$'', ``FSDS if stop''.
Forbidden: inferring entity drill from columns alone.

%======================================================================
\section{Row view: drill / stop}
\label{sec:rows}

\begin{definition}[Row spectrum]
\[
  r_v=\norm{D_{v:}},
  \qquad
  a_v=\frac{\langle D_{v:},\,\delta(S)\rangle}
           {\norm{D_{v:}}\,\norm{\delta(S)}+\varepsilon}.
\]
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Drill gates]
\begin{description}[leftmargin=2.8em]
  \item[D-stop] low $\mathrm{Tail}(r)$ / $\mathrm{CV}(r)$
    (and $a_v\approx 1$): do not refine entity key.
  \item[D-drill] high Tail or CV:
    $K=\mathrm{Top}(r)\cap\{\pi_v\ge\pi^\star\}$
    ($\pi_v=$ probe/bootstrap TopK frequency).
  \item[D-check]
    \[
      R(K)
      =\frac{\norm{\delta(S\setminus K)}^2}{\norm{\delta(S)}^2+\varepsilon}.
    \]
    Accept iff $R(K)\le R^\star$; else reject false tip.
\end{description}
\end{definition}

\paragraph{Rates.}
\[
  \alpha=\frac{|S_K|}{|S|},\quad
  \gamma=1-R(K),\quad
  \mathrm{eff}=\frac{\gamma}{\alpha+\varepsilon}.
\]
Early stop when further shrink yields $\gamma\approx 0$.
Business cap truncates delivery depth independently of $\gamma$.

%======================================================================
\section{When to drill further (argument)}
\label{sec:when-drill}

Drill is not the default. It is justified only when the
\emph{same} conditional-mean object says the parent support is still
heterogeneous under the next entity key.

\paragraph{Necessary condition (parent still shifted).}
If $\norm{\delta(S)}$ is negligible (G0), there is nothing to
localize: stop. Column concentration alone (large $\delta$, sharp
$J^\star$) only licenses a feature story / FSDS \emph{at $S$}; it does
not license a finer entity cut.

\paragraph{Sufficient condition (rows are not homogeneous).}
Compute $\{r_v\}$ on the next key inside $S$. Further drill is
warranted only if the row spectrum is sharp---high $\mathrm{Tail}(r)$
or $\mathrm{CV}(r)$---so that a few values carry most of the
mean-shift. If $r$ is flat and $a_v\approx 1$, every entity looks like
the parent $\delta$: shrinking support cannot raise capture rate
$\gamma$, so stop and (if G1) run FSDS on $S$.

\paragraph{Acceptance, not nomination.}
A sharp $r$ only proposes $K=\mathrm{Top}(r)\cap\{\pi\ge\pi^\star\}$.
Accept the drill iff residual mean-shift drops,
$R(K)\le R^\star$ (equivalently $\gamma$ large enough at tolerable
$\alpha$). If $R\not\downarrow$, the tip is false---revert and stop.
Mass / $\pi$ failures likewise block delivery even when $r$ looks
sharp.

\paragraph{What does not justify drill.}
Feature-only $\delta$ tips; $\mathrm{Var}(\hat\tau)$; community /
ego / graph-local backends; ``effects look stronger downstairs.''
Those are out of scope or the wrong functional.

\paragraph{Rule.}
\[
  \mathrm{Drill}
  \;=\;
  \mathbf{1}\{\norm{\delta(S)}\ge\varepsilon_\delta\}
  \cdot
  \mathbf{1}\{\mathrm{Tail}(r)\ge\tau\ \lor\ \mathrm{CV}(r)\ge c^\star\}
  \cdot
  \mathbf{1}\{R(K)\le R^\star\}
  \cdot
  \mathbf{1}\{\mathrm{mass}/\pi\ \mathrm{ok}\}.
\]
Else $K^\star\leftarrow S$ (or last accepted support). Business cap may
forbid delivery even when $\mathrm{Drill}=1$.

%======================================================================
\section{Feature--entity joint (optional order on same $D$)}
\label{sec:joint}

\begin{description}[leftmargin=1.8em]
  \item[$\mathrm{F}\to\mathrm{E}$ (default)]
    Restrict to $J^\star$ columns, then $r_v=\norm{D_{v,J^\star}}$;
    drill on that $r$.
  \item[$\mathrm{E}\to\mathrm{F}$]
    Full-dim $r$ $\to K$, then recompute $\delta(K)$, $J^\star(K)$.
\end{description}
Declare order; do not silently union two orders.
Multiple entity keys (nested merchant$\to$user$\to$order, or sibling
keys) each get their own $D^{(k)}$; compare $\mathrm{eff}$ and $\pi$;
report \texttt{drill\_key}.

%======================================================================
\section{What $K^\star$ is}
\label{sec:kstar}

\begin{definition}[$K^\star$]
The final support after cmean early-stop (and optional accepted drills)
on graph-feature $X$: the edge set (or entity-id set) on which
\textbf{one} FSDS pass is run.
\end{definition}

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}ll@{}}
\toprule
$K^\star$ \textbf{is} & $K^\star$ \textbf{is not} \\
\midrule
retrieval subset on graph features
  & output of a graph algorithm \\
cmean ($+\pi$/mass) shrunk support
  & community / ego / ULS / PPR kernel \\
delivery edge/entity ids for FSDS
  & causal kernel / effect subgroup \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

%======================================================================
\section{FSDS (once)}
\label{sec:fsds}

On edges in $K^\star$ only:
\[
  \mathrm{StandardScaler}
  \to\mathrm{VarianceThreshold}
  \to\mathrm{SelectKBest}
  \to\mathrm{HGB/LR}
  \Rightarrow\text{feature ranking}.
\]
Do not re-run FSDS at every exploratory layer.
If stop under G0, FSDS may be skipped.

%======================================================================
\section{Optional side score}
\label{sec:po}

Edge scores $s\in\R^n$ from a \emph{single} period-score fit may be
aggregated $g_v=\mathrm{mean}(s\mid e=v)$. Concordant sharpness with
$r_v$ adds confidence; by default $g$ does not overturn D-stop.
Forbidden: $\mathrm{Var}_x(\hat\tau(x))$ as heterogeneity;
per-entity PO refits.

\section{Algorithm}
\label{sec:algo}

\begin{verbatim}
# X: graph features; W: period; e: entity key; S: parent edges
fit StandardScaler on W1; transform X
delta = mean(X|S,W=1) - mean(X|S,W=0)
J_star = top_share(delta)                 # column guidance

for v in mass_ok(e, S):
    D[v] = mean(X|v,W=1) - mean(X|v,W=0)
r[v] = ||D[v]||   # or ||D[v, J_star]|| if F->E

if flat(r):  K_star = S; maybe FSDS(S) if ||delta|| large
else:
    K = top(r) intersect stable_pi
    R = ||delta(S\K)||^2 / ||delta(S)||^2
    if R <= R_star: S <- edges in K; recurse or stop at business cap
    else: K_star = S   # reject false tip
FSDS once on K_star
\end{verbatim}
