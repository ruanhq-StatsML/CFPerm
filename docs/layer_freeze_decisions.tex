\paragraph{What we decide.}
The board does not choose \emph{when} to update---that remains business logic.
It answers a narrower question: \emph{from which layer to freeze training} on an incoming batch, and only as a heuristic readout.

\paragraph{Three series, one reference window.}
Let $D_{\mathrm{ref}}$ be a fixed $T{=}0$ window ($n_{\mathrm{ref}}{=}10^4$).
Each new batch is $T{=}1$.
We read three numbers against $D_{\mathrm{ref}}$, never against a rolling history:
\begin{itemize}\setlength{\itemsep}{2pt}
\item \textbf{PO-risk} (concept / $P(Y\mid X)$).
Own RF nuisances $\mu(Y\mid X)$ and $e(T\mid X)$; $\varphi=(Y-\mu)(T-e)$; risk $=\mathbb{E}[\hat\tau(X)^2]$.
Not the serving MLP, not $\ell_2$.
RF PO-risk is not expected to collapse first.
\item \textbf{Serving MSE} (does the current model still pay rent?).
Evaluated \emph{before} the online step.
This is the series more likely to break first.
\item \textbf{MMD} (covariate / $P(X)$).
Fixed口径: $\mathrm{MMD}^2(X_{\mathrm{new}},X_{\mathrm{ref}})$, RBF, median bandwidth pinned on $D_{\mathrm{ref}}$.
\emph{Not} mean pairwise MMD vs all previous batches, and \emph{not} last-batch layer representations.
\end{itemize}
A series is ``broken'' if its causal moving average is $\ge 2\times$ its $D_{\mathrm{ref}}$-split baseline.
No online-bootstrap.

\paragraph{Heuristic action.}
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{cccc}
\toprule
PO-risk & MSE & MMD vs $D_{\mathrm{ref}}$ & action \\
\midrule
quiet & quiet & --- & keep training every layer \\
broken & holds & --- & watch; do not freeze yet \\
broken & broken & --- & freeze that layer's training \\
holds & broken & broken & $X$-shift; not concept drift \\
holds & broken & quiet & tricky; freeze nothing \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
Freeze-depth clones ($k{+}1$ models, $\mathrm{model}_i$ trains only the top $i$ groups) run \emph{only} on the both-broken cell.
PO-broken / MSE-holds is watch: RF PO-risk should not snap first; keep observing.
MSE-broken / PO-holds is \emph{not} concept drift---read MMD vs $D_{\mathrm{ref}}$.

\paragraph{When the stream moved.}
A frozen $\mathtt{RandomForestRegressor}$ fit once on $D_{\mathrm{ref}}$:
\[
\hat y=\mathrm{rf.predict}(X_{\mathrm{new}}),\qquad
T=\mathrm{MSE}(Y_{\mathrm{new}},\hat y)-E_{\mathrm{ref}}.
\]
The first last-two hop on $T$ is the jump onset.
A slow walk may never fire the $1.5\times$ hop; then read the $T$ and MMD trends.
Same one-liner as MMD: score the new batch against the frozen reference, nothing else.

\paragraph{What we would actually do.}
If both PO-risk and MSE are quiet: full backprop, no freeze.
If only PO-risk moves: watch, do not freeze.
If MSE moves and MMD vs $D_{\mathrm{ref}}$ is large: treat as $P(X)$ shift, do not freeze layers as if $P(Y\mid X)$ hopped.
If both PO-risk and MSE break: freeze training of the bottom groups and keep only the top $i^\star$ trainable.
Do not shrink the batch to invent an update clock; do not bootstrap the MLP.

\paragraph{Next shape: modality LOGO.}
If $X$ is grouped (video / audio / text), leave-one-group-out shares on serving loss and on PO vs MMD localize \emph{which tower} the five cells apply to.
They are not a unique decomposition.
A quiet full-batch cell can still have a hot subset; localize the slice first.
\texttt{train\_top} on a PO-loud tower, \texttt{train\_stem} on an MMD-loud tower.

\paragraph{Gradual / continuous time.}
If \(\alpha(\tau)\) walks, last-two hop ratios go to 1 and the 2$\times$ PO gate may never leave keep.
WHEN is the level of FrozenRF $T(\tau)$ (and serving Brier) against the reference, not $T(\tau)/T(\tau-W)$.
Evaluable latency is excess serving loss while waiting, plus windowing delay and compute lag---not a single $\hat\tau-\tau_0$.
Default action is watch; freeze-from-layer waits for serving error to walk, not for a hop.

\paragraph{Serving error vs share error.}
Hop and continuous time are the same score $S(\tau)$.
The coupling that needs latency is serving Brier/MSE versus the LOGO pointer.
Share-only $\to$ watch (do not freeze a tower). Serve-only $\to$ may update, but do not freeze-from-layer by share.
Both $\to$ \texttt{train\_top} on that tower.
Evaluate the wait by excess serving loss of delayed-update policies, not by which detector fired first.

\paragraph{Sliding-window feature bank.}
If compute allows, keep rolling statistics on a window and score them against the same frozen $D_{\mathrm{ref}}$: PCA reconstruction via a sliding $C\leftarrow C\pm X^\top X$ update (global and per-group), score drift, MMD vs ref, FrozenRF $T$, serving Brier.
These are the clever covariates: concat the bank vector into a shadow / monitoring model. Per-group recon is the cheap $P(X)$ pointer; PO/LOGO stays the $P(Y\mid X)$ pointer.
