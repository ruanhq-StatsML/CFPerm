\section{FICO HELOC CFPerm supplement}
\label{sec:fico-heloc-cfperm}

\paragraph{Motivation.}
The HELOC credit book is not driven by a single strong axis.
Risk performance typically reflects \emph{several weak} covariates acting jointly.
A median cut on one logistic coefficient therefore understates the regime that
CFPerm is meant to handle, while a multi-weak nonlinear concept drift matches
the intended justification.
We report both designs on the cleaned FICO HELOC extract
($n_{\mathrm{raw}}=10459$, $n_{\mathrm{clean}}=9861$ after dropping code $-9$),
with CFPerm settings $B=150$ trees and $n_{\mathrm{perm}}=200$ batch permutations,
subsampling at most $1500$ units per arm.

\paragraph{Design A: median-split covariate shift.}
Fit $\mathrm{RiskPerformance}\sim X$ by $\ell_2$-logistic regression and take the
top-$3$ features by absolute coefficient
($\mathtt{NumInqLast6M}$, $\mathtt{NumInqLast6Mexcl7days}$, $\mathtt{ExternalRiskEstimate}$).
For each feature $j^\star$, partition on the sample median into exist ($W=0$) and
new ($W=1$). This induces a strong one-dimensional covariate shift along $j^\star$
(and near-nonoverlap of that coordinate across batches).
CFPerm is run with VIMP notions
$\{\mathrm{LOCO},\,\mathrm{PermuCATE},\,\mathrm{GRF}\}$
(PermuCATE uses random-forest outcome / tau / conditional models).
Rejection uses the two-level quantile rule on permutation VIMPs;
recovery is whether $j^\star$ enters the rejected set (or top ranks).

\paragraph{Design B: weak-5 + highly nonlinear concept drift.}
Independently for each of $R=5$ draws, sample a set $S$ of $K=5$ features uniformly,
random-split the book into exist/new (shared $P(X)$), keep observational labels on
exist, and on the new arm redraw
\begin{equation}
  Y\mid X
  \;\sim\;
  \mathrm{Bernoulli}\!\bigl(
    \sigma\bigl(\lambda\, g(Z_S)\bigr)
  \bigr),
  \qquad
  \lambda=2.5,
\end{equation}
where $Z_S$ are standardized coordinates in $S$ and
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
  g(z)
  &=
  \sin(\pi z_0 z_1)
  + \tanh(z_2^2 z_3)
  - \cos(z_4)\,z_0
  \\
  &\quad
  + \tfrac12 z_1 z_2 z_3
  + 0.35\, z_4^3
  + 0.25\, \sin(z_0+z_2-z_4)\, z_1.
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
Each coordinate alone is weak; the joint nonlinear map drives the change in
$P(Y\mid X)$.
Power metrics: rejection rate across draws$\times$VIMPs; any true driver in the
top-$5$ VIMP list; mean recall@$5$ of the five drivers.

\paragraph{Post-hoc subset localization.}
After ranking by $\Delta\mathrm{PFI}=\mathrm{PFI}_{\mathrm{new}}-\mathrm{PFI}_{\mathrm{exist}}$
(Ridge permutation importance), cut on the top driver $j^\star$ at its pooled median
and report bootstrap $\Delta\mathrm{VIMP}$ intervals and $\Delta\mathrm{MSE}$ on the
low/high arms (\texttt{subset\_on\_driver}).
Z-cuts further report SMD together with PSI / TVD / Hellinger on selected features
(\texttt{subset\_localize}).
This names the actionable subset; it does not claim an identifiable CS/CD decomposition.

\paragraph{Results.}
Table~\ref{tab:fico-cfperm-power} is the headline comparison.
Under Design~A the overall reject rate is only $0.222$; inquiry median cuts never
reject, and only GRF recovers the continuous $\mathtt{ExternalRiskEstimate}$ axis
(Table~\ref{tab:fico-median-cells}).
Under Design~B the reject rate rises to $0.867$, every VIMP places at least one
true driver in its top-$5$ on every draw, and mean recall@$5$ is about $0.49$
(roughly $2.4$--$2.6$ of the five weak drivers).
PermuCATE and GRF reject on all five draws; LOCO rejects on $3/5$.

\paragraph{Subset insights.}
Table~\ref{tab:fico-subset} summarizes the localization package.
On median splits, Hellinger/PSI on the split-axis bins approach the maximum
(near-disjoint supports), and $\Delta\mathrm{MSE}$ is heavily one-sided
(high/low ratio $\approx 7$).
On weak-$5$ CD, $P(X)$ is shared, so Hellinger stays moderate ($\sim 0.3$--$0.6$);
all five draws concentrate $\Delta\mathrm{VIMP}$ on the \emph{high} arm of the
PFI-top cut, and more true drivers enter the PFI top-$5$ ($9$ vs $3$ row-hits).
The actionable object is therefore the named subset (low/high of $j^\star$), not a
unique CS/CD decomposition.

\paragraph{Takeaway.}
A single strong median axis is a harsh, low-power foil on HELOC.
The multi-weak nonlinear concept-drift design matches the data-generating intuition
and yields clear CFPerm power plus coherent post-hoc subset localization.
Artifacts live under \texttt{results/fico\_heloc\_cfperm/}
(including \texttt{weak\_cd/} and \texttt{subset\_insights/}).
