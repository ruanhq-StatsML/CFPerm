\paragraph{Attribution as a next-batch training adapter.}
FSDS / TSS already return a typed hop $(\hat c,\hat\delta)$ on the batch
relationship board.
The adapter does not invent a loss: it turns that hop into
\emph{training weights} for batch $t{+}1$.

\emph{Heatmap $\to$ sample weights.}
Let $\bar x_s$ be the mean TF-IDF of category $s$.
The causal proxy for the arriving domain is the last observed mean
$\bar x_{t-1}$. Closed-form ridge on all past rows uses
\begin{equation}
  w_s
  =
  \exp\bigl(\gamma(\cos(\bar x_s,\bar x_{t-1})-1)\bigr),
\end{equation}
i.e.\ the same $\mathrm{cosine}(B_i,B_j)$ board that FSDS already plots,
as importance weights (Shimodaira / covariate-shift IW, with the heatmap as
the density-ratio surrogate on category means).
On Amazon Reviews 2023 this hop-weighted ridge is the strongest online MSE
cut we measure against plateau RidgeSGD and the Wu instance bank.

\emph{Typed mix.}
The Wu bank (cosine Nadaraya--Watson on stored $(x,y)$) is the right
predictor when $P(Y\mid X)$ is stable and $P(X)$ moved.
Hop ridge is the right predictor when $P(Y\mid X)$ must be re-fit.
The locked TSS signs set the mixture
\begin{equation}
  w_{\mathrm{bank}}
  =
  \frac{1+2\hat c}{1+2\hat c+4\hat\delta},
\end{equation}
optionally blended with an EWMA stack of the two predictors' recent MSE.
Large $\hat c$, quiet $\hat\delta$ $\Rightarrow$ bank up / step down.
Large $\hat\delta$ $\Rightarrow$ bank down / ridge up.
Both quiet $\Rightarrow$ hold.

\emph{Rolling observations.}
Per hop we log $(\hat c,\hat\delta)$, mean/max hop weight, and each
predictor's online MSE.
Those are the online rolling-window statistics that drive the gate; they
are not a second product loop.

\emph{Package baseline.}
\texttt{river}'s PARegressor is the off-the-shelf online linear baseline on
the same stream; on sparse TF-IDF it does not beat closed-form ridge.
The gain is from \emph{attribution-weighted} batch reuse, not from a new
optimizer.

\emph{Multimodal extension --- modality-$\pi$ hop.}
On MSR-VTT the heatmap hop is modality-aware. Let $\bar x_s^{(m)}$ be the
mean of modality block $m\in\{\mathrm{video},\mathrm{audio},\mathrm{text}\}$
in batch $s$, and let $\pi_m$ be that modality's share on the causal hop
(PO-risk VIMP mass blended with RF-Domain share). Sample weights become
\begin{equation}
  w_s
  =
  \exp\Bigl(
    \gamma\sum_m \pi_m\bigl(\cos(\bar x_s^{(m)},\bar x_{t-1}^{(m)})-1\bigr)
  \Bigr).
\end{equation}
With a single text modality this collapses to the Amazon hop ridge.
$\pi_m$ is the \emph{contribution} of modality $m$ to the hop: it tells the
adapter which past batches to trust when the hop is video-driven vs
text-driven. PO-risk VIMP is \emph{not} used to reweight coordinates for
prediction (that raises Amazon MSE); it is used to form $\pi_m$ and to
choose tokens/patches for zero/noise/missing mask attribution
(Table~\ref{tab:po-hop-ridge}).
Amazon remains the scalar-text MSE board for the weight logic;
Table~\ref{tab:attr-adapter-amazon} is the Amazon score.
