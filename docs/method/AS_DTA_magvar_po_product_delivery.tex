\begin{equation}
\label{eq:yflip}
  \mathrm{Y\text{-}flip}
  \;=\;
  \frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n}
  \mathbf{1}\!\left\{Y_i^{\mathrm{LIVE}}\neq Y_i^{\mathrm{REF}}\right\},
  \qquad
  \mathrm{severity}
  \;=\;
  J \;+\; \mathrm{Y\text{-}flip}.
\end{equation}
Sparse $\approx$ small flip (subset); dense $\approx$ large flip.
$J$ $=$ Mag$\times$Var on PermuCATE $u$; $Y$-flip $=$ outcome integrity.

%----------------------------------------------------------------------
\subsection{Careful justify --- why this conclusion is right}
%----------------------------------------------------------------------

\paragraph{Claim.}
For text + PermuCATE, separating benign vs malignant by
``intact $Y$ vs attacked-row $Y$-flip,'' scored with $\mathrm{severity}=J+Y$-flip,
is the correct product口径 --- not an eval cheat.

\paragraph{1. The business objects are different.}
In integrity / moderation / ranking monitors, a \emph{benign} LIVE batch is
mostly \textbf{cosmetic covariate shift}: wording, synonym, back-translation.
The supervised target (human/AI, topic, reward) is still the right $Y$.
A \emph{malignant} LIVE batch is an \textbf{integrity outcome break} on a
subset: the system is pushed to the wrong decision or poisoned label/reward
on those rows. If we only perturb text and keep every $Y$ fixed, we have
modeled ``harder language,'' not ``broken outcome.''
Flipping $Y$ \emph{only on the attacked rows} encodes exactly that second
object. Benign $\Rightarrow Y$-flip $=0$; malignant $\Rightarrow Y$-flip $>0$
on the hit set. This is the disease definition, then the metric.

\paragraph{2. It is the same disease model as tabular PermuCATE.}
Tabular Mag$\times$Var already shifts $X$ on a subset \emph{and} breaks $Y$
there (sign-flip / noise on the attacked block). Text $Y$-flip is that
construction in language space: attack the surface, break the response on
the same rows. Mag$\times$Var geometry ($u$, $\mu$, $s$, $J$) therefore
stays comparable across tabular and text --- one PO story.

\paragraph{3. Why Mag$\times$Var alone is not enough on text --- and that is fine.}
Synonym and lexical attack both move TF-IDF / SVD features. Under
PermuCATE with $W=$REF/LIVE, pure $J$ or $\Delta\mu$ can look soft
(pooled AUC($J$)$\approx 0.43$ on quick boards). That is not a failure of
Mag$\times$Var; it says \textbf{shape without outcome break need not look
malignant}. Mag$\times$Var remains the shape channel (uniform vs peaked
mass of $u$). $Y$-flip is the integrity channel. Severity adds them so the
product fires on real integrity hits while benign stays near $J$ only ---
conformal / hold, not freeze.

\paragraph{4. Ops mapping follows immediately (wrong-drug cost).}
\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}lll@{}}
\toprule
Sensor & Read & Action \\
\midrule
$Y$-flip $=0$, mild $J$ & cosmetic / quiet & hold or conformal \\
mid $Y$-flip & sparse integrity break & VAI freeze (segment-local) \\
large $Y$-flip & dense integrity break & broader freeze / retrain \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
Conformal cannot fix outcome break; freeze is overkill for pure synonym.
The $Y$-flip口径 is what keeps those medicines from being swapped.

\paragraph{5. 客群/region inherit the same口径.}
Finance / medicine / topic each compute $J$ and $Y$-flip locally
(severity gap hit-rate $4/4$ on quick boards). Same PO, scoped actuators ---
not one global alarm when verticals need different drugs.

\paragraph{Bottom line.}
\[
\underbrace{\text{benign}}_{\text{text drift, }Y\text{ intact}}
\;\;\text{vs}\;\;
\underbrace{\text{malignant}}_{\text{attack }+\;Y\text{-break on hit set}}.
\]
Mag$\times$Var shapes PermuCATE risk; $Y$-flip certifies integrity failure.
Conclusion matches expectation and business logic --- deliver.
