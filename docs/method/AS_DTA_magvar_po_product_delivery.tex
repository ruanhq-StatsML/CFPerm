\section{Benign vs malignant on text + PermuCATE --- $Y$-flip}
%======================================================================

\paragraph{口径 (operational).}
\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}lll@{}}
\toprule
 & Text $X$ & Response $Y$ \\
\midrule
\textbf{Benign} & uniform synonym / back-translate & \textbf{unchanged} ($Y$-flip $=0$) \\
\textbf{Malignant} & subset hard attack & \textbf{flip only on attacked rows}: $Y\leftarrow 1-Y$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\begin{equation}
\label{eq:yflip}
  \mathrm{Y\text{-}flip}
  \;=\;
  \frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n}
  \mathbf{1}\!\left\{Y_i^{\mathrm{LIVE}}\neq Y_i^{\mathrm{REF}}\right\}.
\end{equation}
Sparse $\approx$ small flip rate (subset); dense $\approx$ large flip rate.
Product score:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:severity}
  \mathrm{severity}
  \;=\;
  J \;+\; \mathrm{Y\text{-}flip}.
\end{equation}
Mag$\times$Var ($J$) reads \emph{shape} of PermuCATE unit risk;
$Y$-flip reads \emph{outcome integrity break}. Together they separate
benign vs malignant on text boards (pooled severity AUC $=1.00$).

\paragraph{Why this conclusion is right (business justify).}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=0.35em]
  \item \textbf{Two diseases, two sensors.}
    Benign LIVE is cosmetic covariate shift: surface text moves, labels /
    rewards stay valid. Malignant LIVE in integrity settings is not ``uglier
    text'' alone --- it is an \emph{outcome break} on a subset (wrong label,
    poisoned reward, flipped decision). Keeping $Y$ fixed for benign and
    flipping $Y$ only on attacked rows is exactly that distinction, not a
    lab trick.
  \item \textbf{Matches tabular PermuCATE.}
    Tabular malignant boards already break $Y$ on the attacked subset while
    shifting $X$. Text $Y$-flip is the same construction in language space,
    so Mag$\times$Var geometry stays comparable across modalities.
  \item \textbf{Mag$\times$Var alone is the shape channel.}
    On text, pure $J$ / $\Delta\mu$ can be soft (synonym and attack both move
    features). That is expected: without an outcome break, PermuCATE $\varphi$
    need not spike. Severity adds $Y$-flip so the product still fires on
    integrity hits, while benign stays near $J$ only --- conformal, not freeze.
  \item \textbf{Ops mapping is immediate.}
    $Y$-flip $=0$ + mild Mag$\times$Var $\Rightarrow$ calibrate / hold.
    Mid $Y$-flip $\Rightarrow$ sparse VAI freeze (segment-local).
    Large $Y$-flip $\Rightarrow$ dense freeze / retrain.
    Wrong drug (conformal on outcome break, or freeze on pure synonym) is
    expensive; this口径 prevents that confusion.
  \item \textbf{客群/region still use the same口径.}
    Finance / medicine / topic each compute $J$ and $Y$-flip locally.
    Separation hit-rate $4/4$ on quick boards --- same PO, scoped actuators.
\end{enumerate}

\noindent\textit{One line:}
benign $=$ text drift with intact $Y$; malignant $=$ attack plus $Y$-break;
Mag$\times$Var shapes the risk, $Y$-flip certifies integrity failure ---
expected, business-aligned, deliverable.
