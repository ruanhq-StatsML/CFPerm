\begin{abstract}
\noindent\textbf{Verdict:} effects match product intent --- ready to hand over.
PO is $u_i=(\varphi_i-\hat\tau_i)^2$ (PermuCATE RF), \emph{not} $1-\hat p$.
Mag$\times$Var $(\mu,s)$ on that $u$ is a cheap, business-aligned triage proxy;
the same geometry applies \textbf{per 客群/region} (finance / medicine / topic),
not as one global alarm.

\paragraph{Why Mag$\times$Var fits the business.}
Uniform benign shift lifts $\{r_b\}$ together ($\mu\uparrow$, $s$ quiet)
$\Rightarrow$ conformal. Sparse / integrity break makes $\{r_b\}$
heteroscedastic or flips $Y$ $\Rightarrow$ VAI-freeze. Magnitude alone
confuses the two; Mag$\times$Var separates the medicine.
