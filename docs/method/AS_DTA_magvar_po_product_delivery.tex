\begin{abstract}
\noindent\textbf{PO here} $=u_i=(\varphi_i-\hat\tau_i)^2$ from PermuCATE RF
(not $1-\hat p$). Mag$\times$Var $(\mu,s)$ on that $u$ is the product triage
proxy: cheap, causal, and matches ops---stable var + mean lift $\Rightarrow$
conformal; $Y$-break / sparse mass $\Rightarrow$ VAI-freeze.
\end{abstract}
