\paragraph{Amazon MSE prototype.}
The score is online rating MSE: predict the arriving category, then update.
Live shape on the McAuley heads, $n_{\mathrm{per}}=240$:
$X\in\mathbb{R}^{2160\times 128}$ (TF-IDF, vocab frozen on batch~0),
$y\in[1,5]^{2160}$, $\mathrm{batch}\in\{0,\ldots,8\}^{2160}$.
MSR-VTT $s\in\mathbb{R}^{n\times 2049}$ is not scored here.

\emph{bank.}
A Wu memory of stored $(x,y)$. The arriving review is a cosine-weighted
average of stored ratings (Nadaraya--Watson), then the new rows are
enqueued (cap $800$).
\emph{sdc.}
Five star-means, Yu-compensated by the location field
$\bar x_t-\bar x_{t-1}$. Ablation: it does not cut MSE on this TF-IDF stream.
\emph{gpm$_{\mathrm{typed}}$.}
Plateau RidgeSGD with $\Delta w\leftarrow(I-MM^\top)\Delta w$ iff heatmap
$\hat c$ is loud and $\hat\delta$ is quiet.

On four seeds the instance bank is $1.566$ $(0.063)$ against plateau
$1.590$ $(0.068)$. Typed GPM is a wash ($1.593$). Star SDC is $2.289$.
Do not oversell the bank against plateau.
