\begin{abstract}
\noindent
When a \emph{new category} arrives sparsely in a continuous batch, uniform
\texttt{partial\_fit} is drowned by known-class recirculation.
We use FSDS RF-domain VIMP to localize a feature subspace, build soft
\emph{sample weights} and a hard \emph{observation subset}, and adapt with their
product after a label-support boundary check.
On four boards (wine, covertype, 20~Newsgroups-B, breast-cancer), the fused
channel raises mean novel accuracy by $\Delta\approx{+}0.165$ vs.\ uniform;
gains concentrate on the new class, which is the product claim.
\end{abstract}

%----------------------------------------------------------------------
\section{Problem}
%----------------------------------------------------------------------

Standard UDA assumes a shared label set.
\emph{Category shift} breaks that: new classes appear in the stream
(C-GCD) or source/target supports only partially overlap (PDA).
The operational question is not only detection, but
\textbf{cold-start on the new class} without destroying known-class retention.

\paragraph{Claim.}
Membership from FSDS/CFPerm is a \emph{diagnostic} footprint.
Cold-start adaptation should use
\[
w^{\mathrm{fused}}_i
= w^{\mathrm{sample}}_i\cdot w^{\mathrm{subset}}_i,
\]
gated by support overlap; weights are not unconditionally helpful.

%----------------------------------------------------------------------
\section{Pipeline}
%----------------------------------------------------------------------

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Detect:} LIVE/REF error-rise gate (OnlineRFPerm-style).
  \item \textbf{Attribute:} RF-domain VIMP on batch $W\in\{0,1\}$;
        top-$K$ subspace $\to$ membership $m_i\in[0,1]$.
  \item \textbf{Boundary:} Jaccard(label supports)${<}0.2$ $\Rightarrow$
        \texttt{incomparable} (no directed adapt).
  \item \textbf{Fuse:} $m_i\leftarrow\max(m_i,\,\mathbf{1}\{y_i\in\mathrm{private\text{-}live}\})$.
  \item \textbf{Weights:}
        $w^{\mathrm{sample}}_i=1+\lambda_{\mathrm{eff}} m_i$ (soft);
        $w^{\mathrm{subset}}_i$ boosts $\{i:m_i\ge q\}$ (hard subset);
        default $w^{\mathrm{fused}}=w^{\mathrm{sample}}\odot w^{\mathrm{subset}}$,
        tempered when $m$ is diffuse.
  \item \textbf{Adapt:} mix weighted LIVE with known REF replay ($w{=}1$);
        \texttt{partial\_fit} with $w^{\mathrm{fused}}$ (or soft-only under
        \texttt{auto} when concentration is low).
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Auto policy.}
Sharp footprint $\to$ fused; diffuse footprint $\to$ sample-only.
This guards digits/covertype-style boards where hard subset stacking can
hurt the novel class.

\paragraph{Channel ablation (mean $\Delta$ novel vs.\ uniform).}
\texttt{sample\_w} alone $\approx{-}0.007$;
\texttt{subset\_w} alone $\approx{+}0.031$;
\texttt{sample}$\times$\texttt{subset} $\approx{+}0.117$;
\texttt{both\_fused} $\approx{+}0.165$.
Soft $\times$ hard is what moves the new class.

\paragraph{Reading by board.}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{wine:} novel $2/3\to 1$; known flat --- ideal cold-start.
  \item \textbf{cancer:} novel to $1.0$; known dips slightly --- acceptable tradeoff.
  \item \textbf{news20b:} novel doubles ($0.125\to0.25$;
        \texttt{sample\_w\_fused} reaches $0.375$),
        but known/forgetting pay --- high-d text is expensive.
  \item \textbf{covtype:} fused does not lift novel; soft
        \texttt{sample\_w\_fused} reaches $0.619$ ($+0.095$) ---
        prefer auto/sample when concentration is moderate.
\end{itemize}

%----------------------------------------------------------------------
\section{Takeaway}
%----------------------------------------------------------------------

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Membership diagnoses \emph{where}; fused weights adapt \emph{on the new class}.
  \item Product metric $=$ novel acc (+ known retention / boundary honesty).
  \item Fused is default when the footprint is sharp; otherwise soft sample\_w.
  \item PDA: low label Jaccard $\Rightarrow$ incomparable, no directed update.
\end{enumerate}
