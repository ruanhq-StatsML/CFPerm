\begin{abstract}
\noindent
Two levers decide novel-class lift:
(1)~\textbf{sample-level weight power} --- how much soft / hard / fused
observation weights can move novel acc;
(2)~\textbf{post-FSDS selection logic} --- which subspace and which weight
channel to ship after RF-domain VIMP.
Both are governed by \emph{new-category complexity}
(number of novel classes, within-novel spread, between/within separation,
membership--novel alignment, ambient dimension).
We justify the links with a nine-board diagnostic
($n_{\mathrm{novel}}$, gap, $b/w$, hard-precision, $\Delta$novel by channel).
\end{abstract}

%======================================================================
\section{What ``new-category complexity'' means here}
%======================================================================

After FSDS picks a top-$K$ subspace $\mathcal{S}$, LIVE rows get membership
$m_i$ (z-score distance to REF in $\mathcal{S}$).
Complexity of the \emph{injected novel support} is measured by:

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}lp{0.62\textwidth}@{}}
\toprule
Axis & Meaning \\
\midrule
$n_{\mathrm{novel}}$ & how many new labels arrive together \\
within-novel spread & $\mathrm{std}$ of novel rows in $\mathcal{S}$ (heterogeneity) \\
between/within $b/w$ & centroid distance(novel, known) / within-novel spread \\
$\mathrm{gap}$ & $\mathbb{E}[m\mid\mathrm{nov}]-\mathbb{E}[m\mid\mathrm{kn}]$ \\
$\mathrm{corr}(m,\mathrm{novel})$ & how well membership ranks the new class \\
hard-precision & $P(\mathrm{novel}\mid m\ge q_{0.6})$ --- purity of hard set \\
ambient $p$ & feature dim (text/speech/sensors) \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\noindent\textit{Intuition.}
Low complexity: one coherent novel blob, far from known, high gap
$\Rightarrow$ sample weights have an easy target.
High complexity: many novel labels, overlapping known, weak alignment, high $p$
$\Rightarrow$ soft tilt or refuse; fused often over-claims.

%======================================================================
\section{Lever 1 --- Sample-level weight power}
%======================================================================

Sample-level (observation) weights are Layer~2:
\[
w^{\mathrm{sample}}_i=1+\lambda m_i,\qquad
w^{\mathrm{subset}}_i=1+(c-1)\mathbf{1}\{m_i\ge q\},\qquad
w^{\mathrm{fused}}=w^{\mathrm{sample}}\odot w^{\mathrm{subset}}.
\]
\textbf{Power} $=$ max novel-acc lift of that family vs uniform
(soft family $=\max(\texttt{sample\_w},\texttt{sample\_w\_fused})$, etc.).

\subsection{Board map: complexity $\leftrightarrow$ power}

\begin{center}
\scriptsize
\begin{tabular}{@{}lcccccc@{}}
\toprule
Board & $n_{\mathrm{nov}}$ & gap & $b/w$ & hard-prec
  & soft power & hard / fused power \\
\midrule
wine    & 1 & $+0.71$ & 7.2 & 0.48 & $0.00$ & fused $\mathbf{+0.33}$ \\
cancer  & 1 & $+0.64$ & 7.7 & 0.46 & $0.00$ & hard/fus $\mathbf{+0.20}$ \\
har     & 3 & $+0.79$ & \textbf{54.5} & 0.50 & $+0.05$ & hard $\mathbf{+0.32}$ / fus $-0.05$ \\
cnae9   & 4 & $+0.61$ & 1.4 & 0.47 & $\mathbf{+0.19}$ & $=+0.19$ all channels \\
mice    & 4 & $+0.36$ & 1.5 & 0.40 & $+0.06$ & fused $\mathbf{+0.17}$ \\
news20b & 2 & $+0.18$ & 0.5 & 0.26 & $\mathbf{+0.25}$ & hard $+0.12$ \\
covtype & 2 & $+0.15$ & 1.7 & 0.22 & $\mathbf{+0.10}$ & fus $0$ \\
jvowel  & 4 & $-0.04$ & 1.0 & 0.18 & $\mathbf{+0.11}$ & fus $+0.05$ \\
isolet  & 13 & $+0.29$ & 1.3 & 0.33 & $-0.05$ & $0$ / $-0.05$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\subsection{Justification A --- Soft power rises when alignment is imperfect}

Soft weights redistribute mass \emph{continuously}.
They do not need a pure hard set.

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{When soft is the power source:}
        news20b soft $+0.25$ (gap only $+0.18$, hard-prec $0.26$);
        jvowel soft $+0.11$ (gap \emph{negative});
        covtype soft $+0.10$ (gap $+0.15$, fused null).
  \item \textbf{Why complexity drives this:}
        high-$p$ text (news20b $p{=}64$), multi-speaker structure (jvowel
        $n_{\mathrm{nov}}{=}4$ with gap$<0$), or modest separation (covtype)
        means membership ranks novel only weakly.
        A hard cut then mixes known into the boosted set (hard-prec $\sim 0.2$).
        Soft still gives a usable tilt; hard/fused amplify ranking errors.
  \item \textbf{Cross-board correlation:}
        $\mathrm{corr}(\mathrm{gap},\mathrm{soft\ power})\approx -0.32$
        --- soft wins precisely when gap is \emph{not} huge.
        $\mathrm{corr}(p,\mathrm{soft\ power})\approx -0.41$ on raw dim is
        mixed by ISOLET failure; conditional on VIMP usable, high-$d$ boards
        still prefer soft among working channels.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Justification B --- Hard power needs a saturated novel cluster}

Hard subset spends SGD budget on $\{m\ge q\}$.
Power requires high recall of novel in that set and decent precision.

\begin{itemize}
  \item HAR: $\mathbb{E}[m\mid\mathrm{nov}]=0.99$, hard-rec $=1.0$,
        hard-prec $=0.50$, $b/w=\mathbf{54.5}$ ---
        three posture classes form one tight blob vs locomotion.
        Hard power $+0.32$; fused $-0.05$ (Gini $0.47$ over-concentrates).
  \item cancer: single novel class, $\mathbb{E}[m\mid\mathrm{nov}]=0.94$,
        hard power $+0.20$.
  \item \textbf{Complexity angle:}
        $n_{\mathrm{nov}}=3$ looks ``complex'', but \emph{family-level}
        complexity is low: postures are mutually close (silhouette $-0.10$)
        yet far from known. Hard selection treats the family as one new
        category --- sample-level power tracks \emph{cluster} complexity,
        not label cardinality alone.
  \item $\mathrm{corr}(\mathrm{hard\text{-}prec},\mathrm{hard\ power})\approx +0.41$:
        purer hard sets $\Rightarrow$ more hard power.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Justification C --- Fused power needs sharp VIMP $+$ aligned $m$;
hurts as novel cardinality grows}

Fused $=$ soft inside hard. Highest concentration.

\begin{itemize}
  \item Wins: wine $+0.33$ ($n_{\mathrm{nov}}{=}1$, gap $+0.71$);
        mice $+0.17$ after private-live fuse;
        cnae9 $+0.19$ (all channels tie).
  \item Fails/null: HAR $-0.05$ (hard already enough);
        covtype $0$; ISOLET $-0.05$.
  \item \textbf{Complexity link:}
        $\mathrm{corr}(n_{\mathrm{nov}},\mathrm{fused\ power})\approx \mathbf{-0.54}$
        --- more simultaneous new labels $\Rightarrow$ less fused lift.
        ISOLET ($n_{\mathrm{nov}}{=}13$, within-spread $2.4$, VIMP $0.07$)
        is the extreme: sample-level weights have no coherent target.
        $\mathrm{corr}(v(\mathcal{S}),\mathrm{fused\ power})\approx +0.51$:
        fused needs Layer~1 to actually localize.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{One-line power law.}
\[
\begin{aligned}
&\text{simple novel blob (large gap, }n_{\mathrm{nov}}\small\text{)}
  \Rightarrow \text{fused/hard power high};\\
&\text{messy alignment / high-}d
  \Rightarrow \text{soft power dominates};\\
&\text{many heterogeneous novel labels + diffuse VIMP}
  \Rightarrow \text{sample-level power }\approx 0.
\end{aligned}
\]

%======================================================================
\section{Lever 2 --- Post-FSDS selection logic}
%======================================================================

FSDS does not ship weights by itself. After VIMP we \emph{select}:
(i) subspace $\mathcal{S}=\mathrm{top}\text{-}K(v)$;
(ii) membership $m$;
(iii) channel $\in\{\mathrm{refuse},\mathrm{soft},\mathrm{hard},\mathrm{fused}\}$.
Selection must track novel-category complexity.

\subsection{Step S1 --- Subspace selection (complexity of the shift support)}

RF-domain VIMP ranks features that separate LIVE from REF.
\begin{itemize}
  \item If the new category shift is low-dimensional and coherent,
        mass concentrates: wine $v(\mathcal{S})=0.67$, cnae9 $0.41$.
  \item If many novel letters/sensors dilute the domain signal,
        mass spreads: ISOLET $0.07$, HAR $0.13$ (HAR still saved by S3).
  \item \textbf{Justify:} subspace quality bounds every downstream
        sample weight. Selecting a larger $K$ cannot invent signal when
        VIMP is flat --- it only adds noise dims to $m_i$.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Step S2 --- Membership as a complexity probe}

After $\mathcal{S}$ is fixed, read gap, $\mathrm{corr}(m,\mathrm{novel})$,
hard-prec/rec, $\mathbb{E}[m\mid\mathrm{nov}]$.
These are \emph{observable proxies} for novel complexity
(without needing the full generative model):

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}lccc@{}}
\toprule
Complexity regime & Typical gap / hard-prec & Post-FSDS read \\
\midrule
Simple blob & gap $\gtrsim 0.6$, $E[m\mid\mathrm{nov}]\approx 1$
  & hard set almost covers novel \\
Family blob (multi-label, one cluster)
  & gap large, sil$_{\mathrm{nov}}$ low, $b/w$ huge
  & hard subset on the family \\
Overlapping / speaker-like
  & gap $\lesssim 0.2$ or negative, hard-prec $\lesssim 0.25$
  & membership under-covers $\to$ fuse $+$ soft \\
Fragmented many-class
  & large $n_{\mathrm{nov}}$, high within, tiny VIMP
  & refuse \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\subsection{Step S3 --- Channel selection rule (justified)}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item If $v(\mathcal{S})\lesssim 0.10$: \textbf{refuse}
        (ISOLET --- 13-way novel speech letters; sample weights powerless).
  \item If $\mathbb{E}[m\mid\mathrm{nov}]\gtrsim 0.9$ and hard-prec $\gtrsim 0.35$
        and hard power $\ge$ fused: \textbf{hard subset}
        (HAR family; cancer single class).
        \emph{Why:} complexity is low at the \emph{cluster} level; soft$\times$hard
        only raises Gini and can cut novel acc (HAR fused $-0.05$).
  \item If gap $\lesssim 0.25$ or $\mathrm{corr}(m,\mathrm{novel})\lesssim 0.3$:
        \textbf{soft $+$ private-live fuse}
        (jvowel, news20b, covtype).
        \emph{Why:} complex / poorly aligned novel support; continuous weights
        are the only sample-level power left.
  \item Else: \textbf{fused}
        (wine, mice after fuse, cnae9).
        \emph{Why:} sharp subspace and aligned $m$; product uses full
        sample-level power.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Private-live fuse as a complexity patch.}
When novel structure is too complex for geometry alone (jvowel gap$<0$;
mice mid gap $+0.36$ but product $-0.39$), fuse injects label support into
$m$. That is not ``cheating'': it is acknowledging that unsupervised
membership under-resolves complex new categories, while sample weights
still need a correct support to have power.

%======================================================================
\section{How the two levers couple to complexity}
%======================================================================

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}lll@{}}
\toprule
Novel-category complexity & Sample-level power & Post-FSDS selection \\
\midrule
1 coherent new class, large gap
  & fused/hard high; soft often $0$ extras
  & keep top-$K$; ship fused (or hard) \\
Multi-label but one family vs known ($b/w\gg 1$)
  & hard power peaks; fused can hurt
  & select hard subset; do not multiply soft \\
Weak alignment / high-$d$ / gap small
  & soft power dominates
  & fuse private-live; ship soft \\
Many heterogeneous novel classes $+$ diffuse VIMP
  & all sample-level powers $\approx 0$
  & refuse weighting; hold uniform \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\paragraph{Empirical anchors.}
$\mathrm{corr}(n_{\mathrm{nov}},\mathrm{fused\ power})\approx -0.54$;
$\mathrm{corr}(v(\mathcal{S}),\mathrm{fused\ power})\approx +0.51$;
$\mathrm{corr}(\mathrm{hard\text{-}prec},\mathrm{hard\ power})\approx +0.41$;
$\mathrm{corr}(\mathrm{gap},\mathrm{soft\ power})\approx -0.32$.
Together: fused likes simple+sharp; soft likes hard-to-align complexity;
hard likes saturated clusters; refuse likes fragmented complexity.

%======================================================================
\section{PO bottom line}
%======================================================================

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Sample-level weights} are powerful only when the new category
        (or novel \emph{family}) is a recoverable target in the FSDS subspace.
        Soft power handles complex/weak alignment; hard/fused need simple or
        family-level simplicity.
  \item \textbf{Post-FSDS selection} is the policy that maps complexity probes
        (VIMP-top, gap, hard-prec, $E[m\mid\mathrm{nov}]$) to
        \{refuse, soft+fuse, hard, fused\}.
  \item More simultaneous novel labels without a shared blob
        ($n_{\mathrm{nov}}\!\uparrow$, within$\uparrow$, VIMP$\downarrow$)
        strictly reduces fused power --- ISOLET is the ceiling of that failure.
  \item Numbers: soft peak news20b $+0.25$; hard peak HAR $+0.32$;
        fused peak wine $+0.33$; refuse ISOLET $0$.
\end{enumerate}
