\begin{abstract}
\noindent
We justify --- mechanistically and empirically --- \emph{when} FSDS-localized
weights raise accuracy on a \textbf{new category}.
Four conditions (C1--C4) are not heuristics alone: each follows from how
membership is built, how soft/hard weights reallocate SGD mass, and what
the boards measure.
Key diagnostic:
$\mathrm{gap}=\mathbb{E}[m\mid\mathrm{novel}]-\mathbb{E}[m\mid\mathrm{known}]$.
Large positive gap $+$ sharp VIMP $\Rightarrow$ fused/subset wins;
near-zero/negative gap $\Rightarrow$ need private-live fuse or soft-only;
diffuse VIMP $\Rightarrow$ refuse.
\end{abstract}

%======================================================================
\section{Setup and estimand}
%======================================================================

\paragraph{Protocol.}
REF carries only known labels.
LIVE recirculates known rows and \emph{sparsely injects} novel labels.
Adapt with \texttt{partial\_fit}; evaluate \textbf{novel acc} on held-out LIVE
novel rows (known acc / forgetting are costs).

\paragraph{Two layers.}
\begin{align}
\text{Layer 1:}\quad
&v = \mathrm{RF\text{-}VIMP}(X,W),\quad
W=\mathbf{1}\{\mathrm{LIVE}\},
\quad \mathcal{S}=\mathrm{top}\text{-}K(v). \\
\text{Localize:}\quad
&m_i = \mathrm{clip}\!\left(\frac{\|z_i\|_{2}-q_{0.2}}{q_{0.8}-q_{0.2}}\right),
\quad z_i=\frac{X_{i,\mathcal{S}}-\mu_{\mathrm{REF},\mathcal{S}}}{\sigma_{\mathrm{REF},\mathcal{S}}}. \\
\text{Layer 2:}\quad
&w^{\mathrm{sample}}_i=1+\lambda m_i,\quad
w^{\mathrm{subset}}_i=1+(c-1)\mathbf{1}\{m_i\ge q\},
\quad w^{\mathrm{fused}}=w^{\mathrm{sample}}\odot w^{\mathrm{subset}}.
\end{align}
Optional fuse: $m_i\leftarrow\max(m_i,\,\mathbf{1}\{y_i\in\mathrm{private\text{-}live}\})$.

\paragraph{Why weights can help at all.}
Uniform \texttt{partial\_fit} gives every LIVE row equal SGD mass.
If novel rows are few, the update is dominated by known recirculation.
Upweighting high-$m$ (or fused) rows reallocates gradient toward the new
category --- \emph{if and only if} high-$m$ actually tags novel rows.

\paragraph{Diagnostic we use below.}
\[
\mathrm{gap}
=
\mathbb{E}[m_i\mid y_i\in\mathrm{novel}]
-
\mathbb{E}[m_i\mid y_i\in\mathrm{known}].
\]
Also: VIMP-top mass $v(\mathcal{S})=\sum_{j\in\mathcal{S}}v_j$.

\small

\toprule
Board & VIMP-top & gap & $\mathbb{E}[m\mid\mathrm{nov}]$ & $\mathbb{E}[m\mid\mathrm{kn}]$ \\
\midrule

har     & 0.132 & $+0.789$ & 0.994 & 0.205 \\

news20b & 0.309 & $+0.177$ & 0.466 & 0.289 \\
covtype & 0.587 & $+0.148$ & 0.582 & 0.433 \\

\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

%======================================================================
\section{C1 --- Sharp VIMP is the precondition (justified)}
%======================================================================

\paragraph{Mechanism.}
Membership is distance \emph{inside} $\mathcal{S}$.
If $v(\mathcal{S})$ is tiny, $\mathcal{S}$ is an almost-arbitrary $K$-set:
$m_i$ ranks LIVE rows by noise directions, so upweighting high-$m$ does not
target the new category.

\paragraph{Evidence.}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Works when sharp:} wine $v(\mathcal{S})=0.673$, gap $+0.713$,
        fused $\Delta$novel $+0.333$ (known flat).
        cnae9 $0.407$ / gap $+0.613$ $\Rightarrow$ $+0.188$ on every weight channel.
  \item \textbf{Fails when diffuse:} ISOLET $v(\mathcal{S})=0.071$.
        Gap is still $+0.289$ (novel a bit farther in a bad subspace), but
        \emph{no} channel beats uniform on novel ($\Delta\le 0$).
        Justification: gap on a wrong subspace is not actionable ---
        Layer~2 faithfully amplifies a useless ranking.
  \item \textbf{Cut:} $v(\mathcal{S})\gtrsim 0.30$ $\Rightarrow$ weighting in-play;
        $\lesssim 0.10$ $\Rightarrow$ refuse (hold uniform).
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Why HAR is the exception that proves the rule.}
HAR has weak VIMP ($0.132$) but \emph{extreme} gap ($+0.789$):
novel posture rows sit at $\mathbb{E}[m\mid\mathrm{nov}]=0.994$.
A hard threshold therefore isolates almost-pure novel mass even though
feature attribution is weak.
Fused (soft$\times$hard) still fails here (below) --- C1 alone is necessary
but not sufficient for \emph{fused}.

%======================================================================
\section{C2 --- Channel must match footprint shape (justified)}
%======================================================================

\subsection{Why soft, hard, and fused do different jobs}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Soft} $w=1+\lambda m$: continuous reweighting.
        Tolerates imperfect ranking; rarely zeros known rows.
        Safe when gap is modest or membership partially wrong.
  \item \textbf{Hard} $w=c$ on $\{m\ge q\}$: spends almost all novel budget
        on the top quantile.
        Wins when novel is \emph{tightly} packed at high $m$ (large gap,
        $\mathbb{E}[m\mid\mathrm{nov}]\approx 1$).
  \item \textbf{Fused} product: soft ranking \emph{inside} the hard set.
        Highest Gini (typically $\sim 0.42$--$0.47$).
        Wins when both (i) the hard set is mostly novel and (ii) soft order
        inside it is meaningful --- otherwise it over-concentrates and
        starves useful known replay / boundary novel rows.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Regime A --- Ship fused (sharp $+$ large gap $+$ fuse OK)}

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}lcccc@{}}
\toprule
Board & VIMP & gap & $\Delta$novel fused & known $\Delta$ \\
\midrule
wine   & 0.673 & $+0.713$ & $+0.333$ & $0.000$ \\
cancer & 0.313 & $+0.635$ & $+0.200$ & $-0.028$ \\
cnae9  & 0.407 & $+0.613$ & $+0.188$ & $-0.167$ \\
mice   & 0.195 & $+0.360$ & $+0.167$ & $-0.196$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\noindent\textit{Justification.}
On wine, novel rows are at $m=1.0$ vs known $0.29$: soft$\times$hard puts
SGD mass exactly on the new class; known unaffected.
cnae9: every channel gives the same $+0.188$ --- the new doc categories are
easy once any membership-aligned weight breaks uniform drowning; fused is
safe default.
mice needs fuse (C3) but once fused, product is best.

\subsection{Regime B --- Ship soft (modest gap or high-d / stacking hurts)}

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}lcccc@{}}
\toprule
Board & gap & fused $\Delta$ & soft-fused $\Delta$ & Why soft wins \\
\midrule
jvowel  & $-0.040$ & $+0.053$ & $\mathbf{+0.105}$
  & membership anti-ranks speakers; only fuse$+$soft helps \\
news20b & $+0.177$ & $+0.125$ & $\mathbf{+0.250}$
  & high-d text; hard subset alone $-0.125$ \\
covtype & $+0.148$ & $0.000$ & $\mathbf{+0.095}$
  & modest gap; fused product null on novel \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\noindent\textit{Justification.}
When $\mathrm{gap}$ is small or negative, a hard cut includes many known rows
(false hard-set) or excludes novel rows.
Multiplying by soft then \emph{amplifies the mistake} (Gini$\uparrow$,
novel mass diluted or misallocated).
Soft alone (after fuse) gently tilts without a brittle threshold.
jvowel is the clean mechanistic proof: $\mathrm{gap}<0$ means geometric
membership prefers known speakers; without private-live fuse, product
$\Delta$novel $=-0.105$; with fuse, soft reaches $+0.105$ and known also rises
($+0.037$).

\subsection{Regime C --- Ship hard subset (extreme gap; fused over-concentrates)}

HAR: gap $+0.789$, $\mathbb{E}[m\mid\mathrm{nov}]=0.994$.
\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}lccc@{}}
\toprule
Channel & novel & $\Delta$novel & weight Gini \\
\midrule
uniform     & 0.636 & $0$ & 0.000 \\
\texttt{subset\_w} & \textbf{0.955} & $\mathbf{+0.318}$ & 0.309 \\
\texttt{sample\_w} & 0.682 & $+0.045$ & 0.293 \\
\texttt{both\_fused} & 0.591 & $-0.045$ & 0.465 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\noindent\textit{Justification.}
Hard subset already selects an almost pure novel set
(novel $m\approx 1$).
Soft$\times$hard further reweights \emph{inside} that set and, after
renormalization, relative to known --- Gini jumps to $0.465$ and novel acc
\emph{falls below uniform}.
So: when the hard set is already saturated with novel, stop; do not fuse.

\paragraph{Auto policy.}
Sharp concentration $\to$ fused; diffuse $\to$ soft.
HAR adds: if subset alone $\gg$ fused on a validation slice, keep subset.

%======================================================================
\section{C3 --- Private-live fuse: when membership under-covers (justified)}
%======================================================================

\paragraph{Mechanism.}
$m_i$ is unsupervised geometry in $\mathcal{S}$.
True novel labels can sit mid-pack (mice) or even on the wrong side of $m$
(jvowel $\mathrm{gap}<0$).
Fuse forces discovered private-live ids into the weight support:
\[
m_i^{\mathrm{fuse}}=\max(m_i,\,\mathbf{1}\{y_i\in\mathrm{private\text{-}live}\}).
\]

\paragraph{Counterfactual evidence.}
\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}lccc@{}}
\toprule
Board & without fuse & with fuse & Fuse justification \\
\midrule
mice
  & product $\Delta$novel $-0.389$
  & fused $+0.167$
  & swing $+0.556$: hard set without fuse grabs wrong high-$m$ known \\
news20b
  & sample alone $-0.125$
  & soft-fused $+0.250$
  & fuse turns a harmful soft tilt into a large novel lift \\
jvowel
  & product $-0.105$
  & soft-fused $+0.105$
  & geometric gap negative; fuse supplies the novel support \\
wine / cnae9
  & already $+0.333$ / $+0.188$
  & same
  & redundant: membership already covers novel \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\noindent\textit{When to require fuse:}
gap modest/negative, or product ablates badly relative to fused.
\textit{When fuse is optional:}
$\mathbb{E}[m\mid\mathrm{nov}]\approx 1$ (wine/HAR/cancer).

%======================================================================
\section{C4 --- Boundary refuse (justified)}
%======================================================================

\paragraph{Mechanism.}
If label supports barely overlap, ``adapt toward LIVE'' has no shared task:
directed \texttt{partial\_fit} can overwrite known decision boundaries for
no transferable gain.

\paragraph{Evidence.}
PDA proxy: overlap $0.1$ ($J\approx 0.125$) $\Rightarrow$ incomparable rate $1.0$,
adapt rate $0.0$.
Overlap $0.4$/$0.8$ $\Rightarrow$ adapt rate $1.0$.
Value $=$ risk avoided (not novel lift).
Operational cut: Jaccard $<0.2$ $\Rightarrow$ \texttt{incomparable}.

%======================================================================
\section{Synthesis: decision rule with justifications}
%======================================================================

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Compute $v(\mathcal{S})$ and gap on LIVE.
  \item If $v(\mathcal{S})\lesssim 0.10$: \textbf{refuse}
        (C1; ISOLET --- gap on bad subspace is not enough).
  \item If Jaccard $<0.2$: \textbf{refuse} (C4).
  \item Fuse private-live into $m$ when gap $<\sim 0.4$ or product ablates
        (C3; mice/jvowel/news20b).
  \item Channel (C2):
        \begin{itemize}
          \item gap $\gtrsim 0.6$ and $v(\mathcal{S})\gtrsim 0.3$ $\to$ fused
                (wine/cancer/cnae9);
          \item $\mathbb{E}[m\mid\mathrm{nov}]\approx 1$ but fused Gini hurts
                $\to$ hard subset (HAR $+0.318$);
          \item gap modest/negative or high-d $\to$ soft fused
                (jvowel/news20b/covtype).
        \end{itemize}
  \item Report novel acc first; known/forgetting as cost
        (mice pays $-0.196$ known for $+0.167$ novel; jvowel soft raises both).
\end{enumerate}

%======================================================================
\section{PO bottom line}
%======================================================================

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Justified core: weights help novel prediction iff high-$m$ (after
        optional fuse) tags the new category --- measured by VIMP-top and gap.
  \item C1--C4 are mechanistic, not post-hoc: subspace quality, weight geometry,
        coverage of novel support, and task comparability.
  \item Numbers: peak wine $+0.333$, HAR subset $+0.318$, cnae9 $+0.188$;
        v6 fused mean $+0.090$; best-channel mean $+0.194$; ISOLET $0$.
\end{enumerate}
