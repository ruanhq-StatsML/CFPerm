\begin{abstract}
\noindent\textbf{Objective:} cold-start the \emph{new class} under category
shift --- without drowning \texttt{partial\_fit} in known-class recirculation.
\textbf{How:} two weight layers.
(1)~\emph{FSDS feature-level} RF-domain VIMP localizes which features moved.
(2)~\emph{Post-localization} membership $\to$ soft \texttt{sample\_w} $\times$
hard \texttt{subset\_w}; default channel
$w^{\mathrm{fused}}=w^{\mathrm{sample}}\odot w^{\mathrm{subset}}$.
\textbf{Incremental value (locked numbers):}
mean $\Delta$novel(\texttt{both\_fused})$={+}0.165$ vs uniform on four boards;
channel stack soft$\to$hard$\to$product$\to$fused adds
${-}0.007\to{+}0.031\to{+}0.117\to{+}0.165$;
C-GCD proxy $\Delta$novel$={+}0.196$; multiseed mean $\Delta$novel$={+}0.072$.
\end{abstract}

%======================================================================
\section{PO logic: two weight layers}
%======================================================================

\paragraph{Problem.}
Category shift (C-GCD / PDA) injects private-live labels.
The product question is not only ``did shift fire?'' but
\textbf{can we cold-start the new class?}

\paragraph{Two weight layers (locked).}
\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}llp{0.48\textwidth}@{}}
\toprule
Layer & Object & What it does \\
\midrule
1. Feature-level
  & FSDS RF-domain VIMP
  & Attributes LIVE$\leftrightarrow$REF on $W\in\{0,1\}$;
    top-$K$ features = localization subspace;
    mild amplify of those coords at adapt. \\
2. After localization
  & Observation weights
  & Membership $m_i$ in the VIMP subspace
    $\to$ soft $w^{\mathrm{sample}}_i=1+\lambda m_i$
    and hard $w^{\mathrm{subset}}_i$ on $\{m_i\ge q\}$;
    default $w^{\mathrm{fused}}=w^{\mathrm{sample}}\odot w^{\mathrm{subset}}$. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\paragraph{Claim.}
Layer~1 diagnoses \emph{where}; Layer~2 decides \emph{whom to upweight}.
Membership is diagnostic; fused observation weights are the adaptation lever.
Gated by label Jaccard (PDA incomparable $\Rightarrow$ no directed update)
and tempered when $m$ is diffuse.

\paragraph{Pipeline (short).}
Detect $\to$ Layer~1 VIMP $\to$ localize $m_i$ $\to$ boundary
$\to$ fuse private-live into $m_i$ $\to$ Layer~2 fused weights
$\to$ \texttt{partial\_fit} (+ known REF replay).
Auto: sharp footprint $\to$ fused; diffuse $\to$ sample-only.

\section{Incremental value --- what exactly moves}
%======================================================================

Product metric $=$ \textbf{novel accuracy} on private-live / injected new
labels. Global acc is secondary (often flat or down) because the win is
concentrated on the sparse new class.

%----------------------------------------------------------------------
\subsection{Headline: absolute and relative novel lift}
%----------------------------------------------------------------------

Baseline $=$ uniform \texttt{partial\_fit}.
Treatment $=$ \texttt{both\_fused} (Layer~2 default).

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}lcccccc@{}}
\toprule
Board & uniform & both\_fused & $\Delta$ abs & $\Delta$ rel
  & $\Delta$ known & $\Delta$ forget \\
\midrule
wine     & 0.667 & \textbf{1.000} & \textbf{+0.333} & \textbf{+50\%}
  & $0.000$ & $+0.033$ \\
cancer   & 0.800 & \textbf{1.000} & \textbf{+0.200} & \textbf{+25\%}
  & $-0.028$ & $+0.127$ \\
news20b  & 0.125 & \textbf{0.250} & \textbf{+0.125} & \textbf{+100\%}
  & $-0.185$ & $+0.120$ \\
covtype  & 0.524 & 0.524 & $0.000$ & $0\%$
  & $-0.071$ & $+0.144$ \\
\midrule
\textbf{mean} & & & \textbf{+0.165} & --- & $-0.071$ & $+0.106$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\noindent\textit{Reading.}
Three of four boards lift novel; wine is free on known ($0$ cost);
cancer pays $-0.028$ known for $+0.200$ novel (gain/cost $\approx 7.2\times$);
news20b doubles novel but pays $-0.185$ known (expensive high-d text);
covtype fused is a \emph{null} on novel --- soft channel wins there (below).

%----------------------------------------------------------------------
\subsection{Channel stack: where the $+0.165$ comes from}
%----------------------------------------------------------------------

Mean $\Delta$novel vs uniform across the same four boards
(\texttt{results/fsds\_cfperm\_weight\_bench}):

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}lrc@{}}
\toprule
Channel (Layer~2) & mean $\Delta$novel & Interpretation \\
\midrule
\texttt{sample\_w} alone
  & $-0.007$ & soft membership alone $\approx$ null \\
\texttt{subset\_w} alone
  & $+0.031$ & hard observation subset starts to help \\
\texttt{sample}$\times$\texttt{subset}
  & $+0.117$ & product without private-live fuse \\
\texttt{both\_fused} (default)
  & \textbf{+0.165} & product $+$ membership$\cup$private-live \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\noindent\textbf{Decomposition.}
\begin{align*}
\underbrace{+0.031}_{\text{hard subset}}
\;+\;
\underbrace{(0.117-0.031)=+0.086}_{\text{soft$\times$hard synergy}}
\;+\;
\underbrace{(0.165-0.117)=+0.048}_{\text{private-live fuse}}
\;=\;
\underbrace{+0.165}_{\text{locked cold-start channel}}.
\end{align*}
Soft alone does almost nothing ($-0.007$).
Hard alone is weak ($+0.031$).
The \emph{product} is the first real jump ($+0.117$);
fusing discovered private-live into membership adds another $+0.048$.
Layer~1 does not appear as a $\Delta$ row: it is the subspace that makes
$m_i$ (hence Layer~2) identifiable --- without VIMP localization the
observation weights have no footprint to follow.

\subsection{Per-board tradeoff: novel gain vs known cost}
%----------------------------------------------------------------------

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}lcccc@{}}
\toprule
Board & $\Delta$novel & $\Delta$known
  & gain/$\lvert$cost$\rvert$ & Best novel channel \\
\midrule
wine    & $+0.333$ & $0.000$  & $\infty$ (free)
  & \texttt{both\_fused} / \texttt{sample}$\times$\texttt{subset} ($1.0$) \\
cancer  & $+0.200$ & $-0.028$ & $7.2\times$
  & \texttt{both\_fused} / \texttt{subset\_w} ($1.0$) \\
news20b & $+0.125$ & $-0.185$ & $0.68\times$
  & \texttt{sample\_w\_fused} ($0.375$) \\
covtype & $0.000$  & $-0.071$ & $0$ (fused null)
  & \texttt{sample\_w} / \texttt{sample\_w\_fused} ($0.619$, $+0.095$) \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\noindent\textit{Policy implication.}
Default ship \texttt{both\_fused} when VIMP mass is concentrated
(wine VIMP-top$=0.673$).
On high-d text (news20b VIMP-top$=0.309$), prefer soft fused
(\texttt{sample\_w\_fused}: novel $0.375$, $\Delta={+}0.250$ vs uniform)
over stacking hard subset.
On covtype, fused pays known without novel gain --- auto$\to$sample
recovers $\Delta$novel$={+}0.095$ at novel $0.619$.

\subsection{C-GCD proxy: closed-loop incremental value}
%----------------------------------------------------------------------

Separate board (\texttt{weighted\_cold\_start.json}, cifar100-style C-GCD proxy,
3 stages). Modes: uniform / membership-diagnostic / weighted (auto fused).

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}lccc@{}}
\toprule
Mode & mean novel & mean known & mean forgetting \\
\midrule
uniform     & 0.196 & 0.610 & 0.338 \\
membership  & 0.196 & 0.673 & 0.279 \\
\textbf{weighted (auto fused)}
  & \textbf{0.393} & 0.649 & 0.332 \\
\midrule
$\Delta$ vs uniform
  & \textbf{+0.196} & $+0.039$ & $-0.006$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\noindent Stage detail (novel evaluated when private-live present):
stage~1 novel $0.25\to 0.50$ ($+0.25$);
stage~2 novel $0.143\to 0.286$ ($+0.143$).
Membership alone matches uniform on novel ($0.196$) --- confirms
``diagnosis $\neq$ adaptation''.
Weighted nearly \emph{doubles} novel ($0.196\to 0.393$) while known
slightly \emph{improves} vs uniform ($+0.039$) and forgetting is flat
($\Delta={-}0.006$).

%----------------------------------------------------------------------
\subsection{Multiseed robustness}
%----------------------------------------------------------------------

Three seeds $\{2026,2027,2028\}$ on the C-GCD proxy:
mean $\Delta$novel(weighted$-$uniform)$={+}0.072$;
mean $\Delta$novel(membership$-$uniform)$={-}0.165$;
mean $\Delta$forgetting vs uniform $={+}0.036$.
The fused/auto channel stays positive on novel under seed perturbation;
membership-only stays non-positive --- again diagnosis vs adaptation.

%----------------------------------------------------------------------
\subsection{Efficiency $n^*$ (budget $\to$ novel$\ge 0.60$)}
%----------------------------------------------------------------------

Budgets $\{4,8,12,16,24\}$ novel samples; target novel acc $0.60$.
All three modes hit $n^*{=}16$ on this board (tie on budget).
At the crossing budget $n{=}16$:
uniform novel $=0.667$;
membership $=0.917$;
weighted $=0.833$.
Weighted dominates uniform on the mid-budget path
($n{=}12$: $0.50$ vs $0.417$; $n{=}16$: $0.833$ vs $0.667$)
--- same $n^*$, higher novel density once the budget is spent.
(Uniform catches up only at $n{=}24$ with $0.917$ vs weighted $0.75$;
large-budget regime is not the cold-start claim.)

\section{Takeaway for PO}
%======================================================================

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Layer 1} (FSDS feature-level) localizes the shift subspace;
        \textbf{Layer 2} (post-localization) upweights new-category-like rows.
  \item Locked incremental value on four boards:
        mean $\Delta$novel(\texttt{both\_fused})$=\mathbf{+0.165}$
        vs uniform; stack
        soft$/$$\text{hard}$$/$$\text{product}$$/$$\text{fused}
        $={-}0.007/{+}0.031/{+}0.117/{+}0.165$.
  \item C-GCD proxy: $\Delta$novel$=\mathbf{+0.196}$ (nearly $2\times$),
        known $+0.039$, forgetting flat; multiseed mean $\Delta$novel$=+0.072$.
  \item Tradeoff is board-dependent: wine free; cancer $7.2\times$ novel/known;
        news20b costly (prefer soft fused); covtype fused null
        (prefer auto/sample, $+0.095$).
  \item PDA low Jaccard $\Rightarrow$ incomparable --- value $=$ refuse bad adapt.
\end{enumerate}
