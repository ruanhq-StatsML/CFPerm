\begin{abstract}
\noindent
\textbf{Claim:} FSDS-localized weights raise novel-class accuracy only under
identifiable conditions --- not unconditionally.
\textbf{Primary evidence:} non-routine boards (HAR / Mice / CNAE-9 / JVowels)
plus legacy checks (wine / cancer / covtype / news20b / ISOLET).
\textbf{Rule of thumb:}
(1)~Layer~1 VIMP must be sharp enough to localize;
(2)~pick Layer~2 channel by footprint shape (fused / soft / hard);
(3)~refuse when label supports barely overlap.
Peak lifts: wine fused $+0.333$, HAR subset $+0.318$, CNAE-9 $+0.188$.
Diffuse ISOLET (VIMP-top$\approx 0.07$) $\Rightarrow$ no novel lift.
\end{abstract}

%======================================================================
\section{Mechanism (one line)}
%======================================================================

Layer~1 (FSDS RF-domain VIMP) finds \emph{where} LIVE moved.
Layer~2 turns membership $m_i$ into observation weights
(soft \texttt{sample\_w}, hard \texttt{subset\_w}, or
$w^{\mathrm{fused}}=w^{\mathrm{sample}}\odot w^{\mathrm{subset}}$).
Primary metric $=$ novel accuracy on the injected new categories.

%======================================================================
\section{When it works --- four conditions}
%======================================================================

\subsection*{C1. Layer~1 footprint must be sharp (precondition)}

If VIMP mass on the top-$K$ features is diffuse, membership is noise and
\emph{no} Layer~2 channel helps novel acc.

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}lccc@{}}
\toprule
Board & VIMP-top mass & Best $\Delta$novel & Verdict \\
\midrule
wine    & $0.673$ & fused $+0.333$ & sharp --- works \\
jvowel  & $0.505$ & soft-fused $+0.105$ & sharp --- works \\
covtype & $0.587$ & soft $+0.095$ & sharp --- soft path \\
cnae9   & $0.407$ & $+0.188$ (any wt) & mid-sharp --- works \\
cancer  & $0.313$ & fused/subset $+0.200$ & mid --- works \\
news20b & $0.309$ & soft-fused $+0.250$ & mid --- soft path \\
har     & $0.132$ & \textbf{subset $+0.318$} & weak VIMP, but hard subset OK \\
\textbf{isolet} & $\mathbf{0.071}$ & $0$ (none beat uniform) & \textbf{diffuse --- fail} \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\noindent\textit{Operational cut.}
VIMP-top $\gtrsim 0.30$ $\Rightarrow$ weighting is in-play.
VIMP-top $\lesssim 0.10$ $\Rightarrow$ do \textbf{not} force weights
(ISOLET negative control).
Between $0.10$--$0.20$ (HAR): only a \emph{hard} subset may still help;
fused stacking is risky.

\subsection*{C2. Pick the Layer~2 channel by footprint shape}

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}p{0.28\textwidth}p{0.28\textwidth}p{0.34\textwidth}@{}}
\toprule
When & Ship & Evidence \\
\midrule
Sharp VIMP $+$ clear private-live
  & \textbf{fused} (\texttt{both\_fused})
  & wine $+0.333$; mice $+0.167$; cancer $+0.200$; cnae9 $+0.188$ \\
Moderate / high-d / stacking hurts
  & \textbf{soft} (\texttt{sample\_w\_fused})
  & jvowel $+0.105$ ($>$ fused $+0.053$);
    news20b $+0.250$ ($>$ fused $+0.125$);
    covtype $+0.095$ (fused $0$) \\
Novel rows form a tight high-$m$ cluster; soft$\times$hard over-concentrates
  & \textbf{hard subset} (\texttt{subset\_w})
  & HAR $0.636\to 0.955$ ($+0.318$); fused dips $-0.045$ \\
Diffuse VIMP or PDA low Jaccard
  & \textbf{refuse} (uniform / incomparable)
  & ISOLET; PDA overlap $0.1$ refuse rate $1.0$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\noindent Auto policy already encodes the soft-vs-fused fork
(sharp $\to$ fused; diffuse $\to$ sample).
HAR shows an extra fork: when hard subset alone dominates, do not multiply
by soft.

\subsection*{C3. Private-live fuse matters when membership under-covers the new class}

Membership is a geometric score in the VIMP subspace --- it can miss
true novel rows. Fusing $m_i\leftarrow\max(m_i,\,1\{y_i\in\mathrm{private\text{-}live}\})$
is what turns diagnosis into cold-start.

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}lccc@{}}
\toprule
Board & product (no fuse) $\Delta$novel & fused $\Delta$novel & Fuse value \\
\midrule
mice    & $-0.389$ & $+0.167$ & \textbf{essential} ($+0.556$ swing) \\
news20b & $+0.125$ (sample alone $-0.125$)
        & soft-fused $+0.250$ & fuse rescues soft \\
wine    & $+0.333$ & $+0.333$ & redundant (already sharp) \\
cnae9   & $+0.188$ & $+0.188$ & redundant \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\noindent\textit{When fuse helps most:}
new class is sparse and membership ranks it only partially
(mice biology; noisy text). Skip worrying about fuse only when product
already saturates novel (wine / cnae9).

\subsection*{C4. Boundary honesty --- refuse when supports barely overlap}

PDA proxy (OfficeHome-style):
overlap $0.1$ $\Rightarrow$ incomparable rate $1.0$, adapt rate $0.0$.
This is not a ``failed lift''; it is incremental value as
\textbf{risk avoided} (no poisoned \texttt{partial\_fit}).
Condition for directed cold-start: label Jaccard $\gtrsim 0.2$.

\paragraph{Cost side (when ``good on novel'' still hurts known).}
mice fused: known $-0.196$ for novel $+0.167$ --- acceptable only if novel
cold-start is the product KPI.
cnae9 fused: known $-0.167$ for novel $+0.188$.
jvowel soft-fused: known \emph{up} $+0.037$ with novel $+0.105$ --- ideal.
wine fused: known flat --- free lunch.

%======================================================================
\section{Decision checklist for PO / ops}
%======================================================================

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Gate:} LIVE/REF error rise fires?
  \item \textbf{C1:} VIMP-top mass $\gtrsim 0.30$?
        If $\lesssim 0.10$ $\to$ hold (uniform). If $0.10$--$0.20$ $\to$ try hard subset only.
  \item \textbf{C4:} label Jaccard $\ge 0.2$? Else \texttt{incomparable}.
  \item \textbf{C3:} fuse membership with private-live / discovered ids.
  \item \textbf{C2:} concentration sharp $\to$ fused;
        diffuse or high-d text/speaker $\to$ soft;
        tight high-$m$ cluster with fused regressing $\to$ hard subset.
  \item \textbf{Readout:} report novel acc first; known/forgetting as cost.
\end{enumerate}

%======================================================================
\section{PO bottom line}
%======================================================================

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Effects match expectation \emph{when} Layer~1 localizes and Layer~2
        channel matches footprint shape.
  \item Best regimes: sharp fused (wine/mice/cnae9/cancer), soft
        (jvowel/news20b/covtype), hard subset (HAR).
  \item Failure regime: diffuse VIMP (ISOLET) or PDA incomparable --- do not force.
  \item Numbers to remember: peak $+0.333$ / $+0.318$; v6 fused mean $+0.090$;
        best-channel mean $+0.194$.
\end{enumerate}
