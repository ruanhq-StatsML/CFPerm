\begin{abstract}
\noindent
\textbf{One claim:} under category shift, fused observation weights cold-start
the \emph{new class} better than uniform \texttt{partial\_fit}.
\textbf{One primary number:} mean novel-acc lift
$\Delta\mathrm{novel}={+}0.165$ on four boards.
\textbf{How:} FSDS feature-level VIMP localizes \emph{where}; after that,
$w^{\mathrm{fused}}=w^{\mathrm{sample}}\odot w^{\mathrm{subset}}$ upweights
\emph{whom}. Soft alone $\approx 0$; hard alone weak; product$+$fuse is the gain.
\end{abstract}

%======================================================================
\section{Argument in five steps}
%======================================================================

\paragraph{Step 0 --- Setup.}
New labels arrive sparsely in LIVE (C-GCD / PDA).
Uniform \texttt{partial\_fit} is dominated by known-class rows.
We need a weight channel that spends gradient on the new class.

\paragraph{Step 1 --- Two layers (mechanism).}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Layer 1 (FSDS feature-level):}
        RF predicts batch $W\in\{0,1\}$ $\to$ VIMP $\to$ top-$K$ features.
        This answers \emph{where} the shift lives.
  \item \textbf{Layer 2 (after localization):}
        distance in that subspace $\to$ membership $m_i$;
        soft $w^{\mathrm{sample}}_i=1+\lambda m_i$,
        hard $w^{\mathrm{subset}}_i$ on high-$m$ rows;
        default $w^{\mathrm{fused}}=w^{\mathrm{sample}}\odot w^{\mathrm{subset}}$.
        This answers \emph{whom} to upweight.
\end{itemize}
Membership alone is diagnosis. Fused weights are the adaptation lever.

\paragraph{Step 2 --- What ``incremental value'' means here.}
\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}ll@{}}
\toprule
Term & Definition \\
\midrule
Baseline & uniform \texttt{partial\_fit} (flat weights) \\
Treatment & \texttt{both\_fused} (Layer~2 default) \\
Primary metric & \textbf{novel acc} on private-live / injected new labels \\
Secondary & known retention, forgetting (cost, not the claim) \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
We do \emph{not} sell global acc. The product win is on the new class.

\paragraph{Step 3 --- Primary result (the number).}
Four boards, same protocol. Mean absolute lift:

\[
\overline{\Delta\mathrm{novel}}
=
\frac{0.333+0.200+0.125+0.000}{4}
=
\mathbf{+0.165}.
\]

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}lcccc@{}}
\toprule
Board & uniform novel & fused novel & $\Delta$novel & one-line read \\
\midrule
wine    & 0.667 & 1.000 & \textbf{+0.333} & free: known flat \\
cancer  & 0.800 & 1.000 & \textbf{+0.200} & cheap: known $-0.028$ \\
news20b & 0.125 & 0.250 & \textbf{+0.125} & doubles; known costly \\
covtype & 0.524 & 0.524 & $0.000$ & fused null; use soft \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\noindent\textit{Clear statement:}
on 3/4 boards fused raises novel acc; mean lift $+0.165$;
the one miss (covtype) is handled by auto$\to$sample ($+0.095$), not by forcing fused.

\paragraph{Step 4 --- Why $+0.165$ is not a black box (ablation).}
Same boards, mean $\Delta$novel vs uniform:

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}lcc@{}}
\toprule
What we turn on & mean $\Delta$novel & Clear read \\
\midrule
soft \texttt{sample\_w} only
  & $-0.007$ & almost nothing \\
hard \texttt{subset\_w} only
  & $+0.031$ & small \\
soft $\times$ hard (no private-live fuse)
  & $+0.117$ & first real jump \\
\texttt{both\_fused} (product $+$ private-live)
  & \textbf{+0.165} & locked channel \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\noindent\textit{Causal story in one line:}
\[
\text{soft}\approx 0
\;\xrightarrow{\;+\,\text{hard}\;}
+0.03
\;\xrightarrow{\;\text{product}\;}
+0.12
\;\xrightarrow{\;\text{fuse}\;}
\mathbf{+0.17}.
\]
Layer~1 is not a $\Delta$ row because it is the \emph{precondition}:
without VIMP subspace, $m_i$ has no meaning and Layer~2 cannot fire.

\paragraph{Step 5 --- What we pay, and when not to ship fused.}
\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}lccc@{}}
\toprule
Board & $\Delta$novel & $\Delta$known & Decision \\
\midrule
wine    & $+0.333$ & $0$       & ship fused (free) \\
cancer  & $+0.200$ & $-0.028$  & ship fused (7$\times$ novel/known) \\
news20b & $+0.125$ & $-0.185$  & prefer \textbf{soft fused} (novel $0.375$) \\
covtype & $0$      & $-0.071$  & prefer \textbf{auto/sample} ($+0.095$) \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\noindent\textit{Policy (clear):}
default = fused when footprint is sharp;
if diffuse / high-d / fused-null $\to$ soft sample only.
PDA: label Jaccard $<0.2$ $\Rightarrow$ \texttt{incomparable} (refuse adapt).

%======================================================================
\section{Supporting checks (same claim, other boards)}
%======================================================================

These do not replace the $+0.165$ headline; they show the claim is not
one-bench luck.

\paragraph{A. C-GCD closed-loop proxy.}
uniform novel $0.196$ $\to$ weighted $0.393$
($\Delta={+}\mathbf{0.196}$, $\approx 2\times$).
Membership alone stays at $0.196$ --- diagnosis $\neq$ adaptation.
Known $+0.039$; forgetting flat ($-0.006$).

\paragraph{B. Multiseed.}
3 seeds: mean $\Delta$novel(weighted$-$uniform)$={+}\mathbf{0.072}>0$;
membership$-$uniform mean $-0.165$.
Gain stays positive under seed noise; diagnostic channel does not.

\paragraph{C. PDA refuse.}
Overlap $0.1$: incomparable rate $=1.0$ (no directed update).
Value here $=$ risk avoided, not novel lift.

%======================================================================
\section{PO bottom line}
%======================================================================

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Mechanism:} Layer~1 finds features; Layer~2 weights new-class-like rows.
  \item \textbf{Primary incremental value:}
        $\overline{\Delta\mathrm{novel}}(\texttt{both\_fused}-\mathrm{uniform})
        =\mathbf{+0.165}$.
  \item \textbf{Why:} soft$\approx$0, hard small, product$+$fuse $=$ the gain.
  \item \textbf{Cost:} usually small on known (wine/cancer); expensive on news20b;
        covtype uses soft, not fused.
  \item \textbf{Guardrails:} auto/temper + PDA incomparable.
\end{enumerate}
