\paragraph{IR probe, not quantile regression.}
OnlineRFPerm's MSE is a pointwise rent check. Ranking systems pay rent in NDCG.
Freeze a scorer on $D_{\mathrm{ref}}$. Each batch is slates. Loss $=1-\mathrm{NDCG}@k$
vs the hold-out slice of $D_{\mathrm{ref}}$. $T$ is the batch label. $Y$ is graded
relevance, last column, never a feature. Slate ids are not $X$.
\paragraph{Read.}
A rank-rule flip should ring NDCG. An unused-column walk should ring MMD and
leave ranking quiet. Quiet FAR on NDCG is the IR analogue of the hop twitch;
ADDIS stays the primary mark if you put NDCG-loss on the $p$-stream.
