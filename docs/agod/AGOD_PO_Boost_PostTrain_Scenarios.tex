\begin{abstract}
We summarize how \textbf{PO-risk} boosts \emph{post-training}:
not by replacing the loss, but by reallocating update budget across
\textbf{scenarios}.
Two resolutions share one sensor---batch/modality $\PO_m$ and
observation $\PO_i$.
Gains are reported as update $\Flops\downarrow$ / faster
$T(\Acc^\star)$ under an Acc constraint, plus reject-gated
next-MSE when sample weights fire.
PO is sufficient here; other attributors are optional subtractors
(MMD) or amplifiers (proto), not replacements.
\end{abstract}

\section{One-line logic}
\[
\PO
\;\longrightarrow\;
\begin{cases}
\text{modality emphasis:} &
  \alpha_m \to \{\mathrm{freeze},\,s_m,\,\lambda_m\}\\
\text{sample weights:} &
  w_i\propto\sqrt{\PO_i}\ \text{only after gate reject}
\end{cases}
\;\longrightarrow\;
\text{faster post-train}.
\]
Train on the \emph{shift}, with Acc as constraint---not Acc chase alone.

\paragraph{Allocation vs $\varepsilon$-greedy.}
Budgeting is Softmax / fused long$\otimes$short (state-dependent), not a
coin-flip explore schedule.
The only $\varepsilon$-flavored knob is the \texttt{po\_budget} floor
($f\approx\varepsilon/|M|$).
Sensor-triggered OOD: gate high-$\PO$ only when uni-Acc/VIMP clear;
then freeze$\leftarrow L$, dump$\leftarrow S$ on window $t{+}1$.
See ContinuousTime roadmap \S\texttt{eps}.

  & $\Flops\sim0.70$--$0.83$ at Acc$\approx$flat \\

  & Short track $\Delta\PO$ dumps steps; long $L$ keeps set stable

  & $\PO-\lambda\mathrm{MMD}$ or gate before spend
  & Do not buy steps for pure $X$-shift \\

  (RFPerm/CFPerm fires)
  & Soft $w_i\propto\sqrt{\PO_i}$ on Fit$_{t+1}$

  (COCO Acc$\sim0.9$)
  & Soft LR / stack prior only; freeze off
  & \textbf{Near-zero FLOPs gain}---do not claim speedup \\

  & \texttt{po\_gated} then Softmax
  & Primary FLOPs saver when $M$ large \\

\paragraph{PO is enough.}
All scenarios above are driven by residual-concept $\PO$
(optionally tempered by MMD/proto/$\Delta$).
No second primary sensor is required for the boost claim.

\section{What ``gain'' means}
\label{sec:gain}

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}llp{5.4cm}@{}}
\toprule
Gain KPI & Role & Note \\
\midrule
$\Flops=\#\{\mathrm{BWD\ on}\}/M$
  & Primary speed
  & FWD stays on; not inference latency \\
$T(\Acc^\star)$
  & Primary speed
  & Windows to hit Acc bar \\
$\Delta\Acc$ vs equal
  & Constraint
  & Pass only if $\ge-\varepsilon$ (e.g.\ $-0.5\%$) \\
Next-MSE drop (reject)
  & Sample-weight gain
  & Only on gated rejects; vs uniform $w$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\noindent\textbf{Ship rule:}
$\Flops\downarrow$ or $T(\Acc^\star)\downarrow$
\emph{and} Acc constraint holds.
Acc$\uparrow$ alone without cost cut is not a post-train boost.

\paragraph{S1 Affec ($M{=}5$) detail.}
Relative to \texttt{equal} ($\Flops{=}1$, Acc$\approx0.508$):
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2em,itemsep=1pt]
  \item \texttt{po\_gated}: $\Flops\approx0.70$, $\Delta\Acc\approx-0.4\%$
        --- best FLOPs cut (gain = update cost).
  \item \texttt{po\_proto}: $\Flops\approx0.83$, $\Delta\Acc\approx+0.2\%$
        --- mild quality+cost gain.
  \item \texttt{po\_soft}/\texttt{po\_delta}: $\Flops\approx0.78$--$0.83$,
        Acc roughly flat.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{S5 Food / Fashion / COCO ($M{=}2$).}
All metric versions $\Flops\approx1.0$, Acc unchanged
$\Rightarrow$ \textbf{no boost claim}; keep equal or soft budget only.

\paragraph{S4 Sample weights (stream packs).}
Gated $\sqrt{\PO}$ beats always-$\sqrt{\PO}$ on the
quality/cost tradeoff ($6/6$ packs in gated bench);
raw $\propto\PO$ overfits the reject batch---soft sqrt is the
default gain recipe.

\paragraph{S2 Long$\otimes$short (\texttt{po\_fuse}).}
Hierarchical actuators: freeze$\leftarrow$long $L$,
step dump$\leftarrow$short $S$, LR$\leftarrow$fused $\alpha$.
Gain vs single Softmax: less thrash + earlier dump on spikes
(instrumented via $\omega_S$, \texttt{top\_concept}/\texttt{top\_spike}).

\section{Takeaway}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.2em,itemsep=2pt]
  \item PO boosts post-training by \textbf{budget reallocation}
        (modality + optional sample weights), not a new loss.
  \item Real gains land on \textbf{S1/S2/S4/S6}; S5 is a known noop---
        report honestly.
  \item Headline Affec gain: $\Flops$ to $\sim0.7$ with Acc held;
        reject $\sqrt{\PO}$ for row-level boost when the gate fires.
  \item PO alone is the sensor; map scenarios $\to$ ROI dims
        (\texttt{po\_posttrain\_roi\_map.sql}) if business tracking is needed.
\end{enumerate}
