\begin{abstract}
\textbf{Claim.}
Propensity / residual-concept risk $\PO$ is enough to \emph{accelerate
post-training} under an accuracy constraint---not by changing the loss
family, but by reallocating update mass on two orthogonal axes:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.2em,itemsep=1pt]
  \item \textbf{Modality (column):} which tower gets BWD / steps / LR;
  \item \textbf{Sample (row):} on rejected (OOD) batches, up-weight
        rows with large $\PO_i$ via $w_i\propto\sqrt{\PO_i}$.
\end{enumerate}
$\alpha$-Softmax is secondary scaffolding.
The advisor-facing core is sample-level:
\textbf{put post-train gradient mass on OOD-large rows, only when the
batch is gated as OOD.}
We collect formulas, the causal loop, ship rules, and all current
numbers (Affec smoke + synthetic continuous gains) in one place.
\end{abstract}

\section{Goal lock}
\label{sec:goal}

Post-training $=$ a stream of mini-windows $(X_t,Y_t)$ after a frozen
reference fit $\hat\mu$.
\textbf{Primary KPIs} (speed): update $\Flops$, cumulative FLOPs to
$\Acc^\star$, wall-clock / $T(\Acc^\star)$.
\textbf{Constraint:} $\Delta\Acc\ge-\varepsilon$ vs dense equal
(e.g.\ $\varepsilon=0.005$).
Acc$\uparrow$ alone without cost cut is \emph{not} a post-train boost.

\[
\boxed{
\PO
\;\longrightarrow\;
\begin{cases}
\text{modality:} &
  \{\mathrm{freeze}_m,\,s_m,\,\lambda_m\}\\[2pt]
\text{sample:} &
  w_i\propto\sqrt{\PO_i}\ \textbf{iff reject}
\end{cases}
\;\longrightarrow\;
\text{faster Fit}_{t+1}.
}
\]

\section{One sensor, two resolutions}
\label{sec:two}

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}llp{5.2cm}@{}}
\toprule
Layer & Object & Concrete rule \\
\midrule
\textbf{Modality}
  & $\PO_m$ (per tower)
  & Freeze low chronic $L_m$; dump steps on spike $S_m{=}\Delta\PO_m$;
    optional LR from fused $\alpha$ \\
\textbf{Sample}
  & $\PO_i=|Y_i-\hat\mu(X_i)|$
  & If batch \texttt{rejected}: $w_i\propto\sqrt{\PO_i}$;
    else $w_i=1$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\noindent\textbf{Do not conflate:}
high $\PO_m$ does \emph{not} by itself up-weight rows;
reject does \emph{not} by itself freeze a tower.
Same PO family, orthogonal actuators.

\paragraph{What $\PO$ is / is not.}
$\PO_i$ $=$ residual under the \emph{control} fit (how far the row sits
from current $\mu$), \textbf{not} intrinsic label difficulty / ``hard
example'' in the curriculum sense.
High $\PO$ can be true concept shift, noise, or bad $\mu$; that is why
a \textbf{batch gate} precedes row weights.

\section{Sample-level: OOD-large rows get weights}
\label{sec:sample}
\textit{(Primary advisor story.)}

\subsection{Four concrete steps}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.2em,itemsep=3pt]
  \item \textbf{Batch OOD event?}
        \[
          \texttt{rejected}
          \;=\;
          \underbrace{\mathrm{RFPerm/CFPerm}}_{\text{preferred}}
          \;\lor\;
          \underbrace{e_t/e_{t-1}\ge\gamma}_{\text{hop OOS, }\gamma=1.5}
          \;\lor\;
          \underbrace{\mathrm{proxy}(\overline{\PO},|\Delta\PO|,\overline{\mathrm{MMD}})}_{\text{fallback}}.
        \]
        If false: $w_i\equiv 1$ (calm windows untouched).

  \item \textbf{Row OOD size:}
        $\PO_i=|Y_i-\hat\mu(X_i)|$
        (optionally blend batch-level $R^{\PO}$).

  \item \textbf{Weight map} (only if rejected):
        \[
          w_i
          =
          \mathrm{clip}\!\left(
            \frac{\sqrt{\PO_i}}{\mathrm{mean}_j\sqrt{\PO_j}},\;
            [0.25,4]
          \right).
        \]
        Code: \texttt{po\_iptw\_weights(..., mode='sqrt')}.
        Soft $\sqrt{\,\cdot\,}$ beats raw $\propto\PO$ (less reject-batch
        overfit). Ablations: \texttt{cbrt}, \texttt{prop}, \texttt{uniform}.

  \item \textbf{Hit post-train Fit} (causal $t\to t{+}1$):
        \[
          \mathcal{L}_{t+1}
          =
          \frac1n\sum_i w_i\,\ell\!\big(f_\theta(X_i),Y_i\big).
        \]
        In compare: \texttt{\_weighted\_ce} when \texttt{used\_reject\_w}.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Why this accelerates post-training}
Uniform CE on a shifting stream spends equal gradient mass on easy
in-support rows and on the rows that carry the shift.
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2em,itemsep=2pt]
  \item \textbf{Gate first (event time).}
        Reweight budget is spent only when the batch is already
        flagged OOD. Always-on $\sqrt{\PO}$ taxes calm packs
        (gated-$\sqrt{}$ wins $6/6$ stream packs in prior benches).
  \item \textbf{Weight OOD-large rows.}
        Large $\PO_i$ $=$ large residual under current $\mu$; those are
        the coordinates where the next update must bite for
        next-window MSE / Acc recovery.
  \item \textbf{Soft $\sqrt{\PO}$.}
        Mean $w=1$ keeps optimizer scale; clip stops single outliers
        from dominating the reject batch.
  \item \textbf{KPI.}
        Report \textbf{next-MSE drop vs uniform $w$} on reject windows
        only (ROI\_B in \texttt{po\_posttrain\_roi\_map.sql}).
        Do not average into calm windows. Acc remains a constraint;
        do not claim inference latency.
\end{itemize}

\noindent\textit{One line:}
post-train under shift is slow when updates chase easy rows;
gated $\sqrt{\PO}$ moves the same step budget onto high-residual rows
\emph{only on reject}, so $T(\Acc^\star)$ / next-MSE improve without
calm-pack tax.

\section{Modality-level (orthogonal)}
\label{sec:mod}

Optional but useful when $M\ge3$.
Long / short split (\texttt{po\_fuse}):
\[
  L_m=\mathrm{EMA}_\rho(\PO_m)\cdot(1+\mathrm{proto}_m),\quad
  S_m=\Delta\PO_m,\quad
  \mathrm{spike\_ratio}=\|S\|_\infty/(\mathrm{MAD}(L)+\varepsilon).
\]
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2em,itemsep=1pt]
  \item Freeze $\leftarrow L$ (BWD off on low chronic towers) ---
        cuts \emph{update} FLOPs and useless gradient variance.
  \item Step dump $\leftarrow S$ --- fixed budget, smash spike tower;
        acceleration KPI, \textbf{not} a FLOPs-save claim.
  \item LR / stack $\leftarrow$ fused $\alpha$ --- secondary.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{FLOPs amount (honest).}
FWD always on; freeze only kills BWD
(\texttt{freeze\_flops\_rel} / \texttt{flops\_rel\_proj}):
\[
\Flops
=
\frac{C_{\mathrm{fwd}}+C_{\mathrm{bwd}}(A)}{C_{\mathrm{fwd}}+C_{\mathrm{bwd}}(M)},
\quad
c_{pb}\approx 2c_{pf}
\;\Rightarrow\;
\Flops\approx\frac{|M|+2|A|}{3|M|}.
\]
Bare $|A|/|M|$ \emph{overstates} savings.

\paragraph{Not $\varepsilon$-greedy.}
Allocation is Softmax / fused state-dependent control, not a coin flip.
Only $\varepsilon$-flavored knob: \texttt{po\_budget} floor
$f\approx\varepsilon/|M|$.

\section{Causal loop (minimal diagram)}
\label{sec:loop}

\begin{verbatim}
window t train under (freeze, steps, LR, w)_{from t-1}
   |
   v
sensors:  PO_m, PO_i, MMD, proto, Delta PO, uni_acc, VIMP
   |
   +-- drift-vs-noise: damp fake high-PO noise
   +-- reject?: RFPerm | hop_oos | proxy
   |
   v
actuators for t+1:
   modality: freeze<-L, step_dump<-S, LR<-alpha   (optional)
   sample:   if reject: w ~ sqrt(PO_i) else w=1   (core)
\end{verbatim}

\section{Takeaway for the next decade of work}
\label{sec:take}

PO gives a \emph{single language} for ``where the shift lives'' at
column and row resolution.
Post-training then becomes a controlled budget problem:
freeze noise towers, dump steps on spikes, and---most importantly---
\textbf{gate then up-weight OOD-large samples}.
The MVP freezes that logic with runnable smoke numbers; scaling the
same sensors to production streams is the long arc.
