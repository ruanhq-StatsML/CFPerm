\paragraph{Setup.}
Blunt serving use-case only: generate with a small CPU model (\texttt{transformers}/\texttt{SmolLM2-135M-Instruct}), label faithfulness vs.\ context, run OnlineRFPerm, route \texttt{model\_rollback\_or\_audit\_topk} / \texttt{retrieval\_refresh}. Quiet batches keep context in the prompt; hop batches drop context and force invent-style decoding. Gate $\gamma=1.25$, $n_{\mathrm{per}}=12$, $B=6$, cut$=3$.

\paragraph{Datasets.}
(i)~\textbf{HaluEval} QA with knowledge snippets (\texttt{halueval\_qa\_3000.jsonl}); (ii)~\textbf{SQuAD} validation contexts (\texttt{squad\_val\_500.jsonl}).
