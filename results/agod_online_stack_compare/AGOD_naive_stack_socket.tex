\begin{abstract}
\noindent
We report a deliberately \emph{naive} use-case for attribution-guided online
adaptation: keep the towers fixed, keep the online protocol fixed, and change
only a simplex fusion weight $w$ via a $\sim$20-line loss socket
\[
  L \;=\; \mathrm{CE}\!\big(\textstyle\sum_m w_m z_m,\, y\big)
        \;+\; \lambda\,\mathrm{KL}(w\Vert\alpha),
\]
where $\alpha$ is an EMA of an external attributor (FSDS / MSG / VIMP),
not an MoE load-balance statistic.
On Affec (5 physio modalities) this socket turns a mean-fusion
\emph{collapse} into a clear gain
($\Delta$MSE $\approx{+}0.09$, $\Delta$Acc $\approx{+}0.09$ vs.\ mean;
another ${+}0.02$ vs.\ free stacking).
Temperature-sharpened stacking ($\tau{=}0.5$) is even stronger.
On COCO ImgTxt the baseline is already strong; soft sockets add little
accuracy but tighten $\|w-\alpha\|$.
The point is portability: any multimodal online learner with an
attribution score and a simplex fusion weight can reuse the same socket.
\end{abstract}

% ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
\section{The naive use-case}
\label{sec:naive}

\textbf{Setup.}
Per window $t$, modality towers emit logits $\{z_m\}_{m\in M}$.
A stacking weight $w=\mathrm{softmax}(\psi)\in\Delta^{|M|-1}$ fuses
$\hat z=\sum_m w_m z_m$.
An external attributor (FSDS / MSG / VIMP) yields a simplex prior
$\alpha_t$, EMA-smoothed across windows.

\textbf{What we do \emph{not} change.}
Tower architecture, step budget, holdout split, learning rate, or the
attribution sensor. Only the \emph{weight socket} changes.

\textbf{Variants (same towers / windows).}
\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}lll@{}}
\toprule
Name & Fusion weight & Extra loss \\
\midrule
\texttt{mean\_ce}      & mean-pool hiddens (no $w$) & --- \\
\texttt{stack\_ce}     & free $w=\mathrm{softmax}(\psi)$ & --- \\
\texttt{stack\_alpha}  & free $w$ & $\lambda\mathrm{KL}(w\Vert\alpha)$ \\
\texttt{stack\_uniform}& free $w$ & $\lambda\mathrm{KL}(w\Vert U)$ \\
\texttt{stack\_fixed}  & freeze $w:=\alpha$ & --- \\
\texttt{stack\_temp}   & $w=\mathrm{softmax}(\psi/\tau)$, $\tau{=}0.5$ & --- \\
\texttt{stack\_erank}  & free $w$ & erank / align (+$\alpha$ if collapsed) \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\textbf{Why this is not MoE aux.}
Classical MoE balance pushes gates toward \emph{internal} uniformity /
load. Here $\alpha$ is an \emph{external} attributor: who carries concept
drift mass. The socket is classical online stacking plus that prior
\cite{wolpert1992stacked}-style fusion with an FSDS/MSG plug.

\section{The twenty-line socket}
\label{sec:code}

The entire actuator (beyond an existing CE on stacked logits) is:

\begin{lstlisting}[language=Python,caption={Attribution prior on stacking weights ($\sim$20 LOC).}]
def alpha_stack_kl(stack_w, alpha, mods, *, lambda_kl=0.5):
    w = stack_w.clamp_min(1e-8); w = w / w.sum()
    prior = torch.tensor([max(float(alpha[m]), 1e-8) for m in mods],
                         device=w.device, dtype=w.dtype)
    prior = prior / prior.sum()
    kl = (w * (w.log() - prior.log())).sum()   # KL(w || alpha)
    return lambda_kl * kl

# in the online window loop:
logits, h, logits_m, w = model(batch, return_parts=True)
loss = CE(logits, y) + alpha_stack_kl(w, alpha_ema, mods)
\end{lstlisting}

Alternate sockets (\texttt{uniform}, \texttt{temp}, \texttt{erank},
\texttt{fixed}) swap only the prior / temperature line; towers stay put.
Code: \texttt{agod/online\_stack.py} (\texttt{WEIGHT\_MODES},
\texttt{stack\_weight\_aux}).

\section{Takeaway}
\label{sec:takeaway}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Naive is enough.} A KL (or temperature) on simplex fusion
        weights---tens of lines, no new backbone---moves holdout metrics
        when mean fusion fails under multimodal drift.
  \item \textbf{External prior $\neq$ MoE balance.} $\alpha$ comes from an
        attributor (FSDS/MSG/VIMP), not from uniform/load stats.
  \item \textbf{Portable socket.} Pattern:
        \[
          \underbrace{\text{attributor}}_{\text{sensor}}
          \;\to\;
          \underbrace{\alpha}_{\text{EMA}}
          \;\to\;
          \underbrace{\mathrm{KL}(w\Vert\alpha)\ \text{or}\ \tau\text{-softmax}}_{\text{actuator}}.
        \]
        Drop into any multimodal online stacker.
\end{enumerate}
